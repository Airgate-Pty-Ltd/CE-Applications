## 1. Corrections to v1 (read first)

- **AS9100D 10.2 is valid.** v1 and the earlier findings listed `10.2` as a seed reference missing from the standard. It is in the licensed table of contents ("10.2 Nonconformity and Corrective Action") and cited in the body ("see 10.2"). The count of invalid AS9100D seed references is therefore **25**, not 26. The AS9110C count of 6 is unchanged. `content/AS-LICENSED-REGISTER-FINDINGS.md` is regenerated from the corrected register.
- **Clause universe counts changed:** AS9100D 93 → **100**, AS9110C 99 → **106**. The earlier register missed the top-level clauses 4–10 because the extracted text has no heading lines for them. The licensed text does have the navigation line "4 5 6 7 8 9 10", which proves they exist. Their **titles are not extracted and are left null**, not guessed.
- **Parent-clause gap fixed.** v1 had 25 subclauses per standard whose parent clause was not in the universe. The register now unions table-of-contents headings and body references (`content/build_register.py`). Orphans: **0** for both standards.
