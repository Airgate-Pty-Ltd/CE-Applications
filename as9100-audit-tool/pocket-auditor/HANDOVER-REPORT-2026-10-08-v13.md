## 5. Leakage found and fixed in this pass

Checking the external sample found three licensed-content paths that the earlier rules did not cover. All are fixed, and tests now cover them.

1. **AS9110C checklist rows, not-assessed rows and assessments were in the external package.** The licensed-standard rule had only been applied to QSA rows. Now the snapshot standard's registry entry decides: if it is `ORG_PRIVATE_LICENSED` or `CUSTOMER_PRIVATE_LICENSED`, its rows leave the package for non-owners.
2. **Findings and CARs carried licensed requirement IDs.** Now replaced by `WITHHELD_FOR_LICENCE` for non-owners.
3. **Photo evidence carried a QSA `requirement_id`.** Found by the sample test. Now replaced for non-owners.

The licensed prefixes come from the registry (`licensed_prefixes`), not from hard-coded standard names, so a future licensed standard is covered by its registry entry.
