Internal Auditor Competency Assessment Tool

Auditor Self-Assessment CPD Planning Assess auditor competency across 6 domains and generate a targeted continuing professional development action plan.

How this works: Rate your proficiency in each competency item on a 0–3 scale. A score of 3 means fully proficient; 0 means not yet developed. Items marked with your selected standard(s) are weighted for that audit scope. The tool produces a domain-by-domain competency profile, QUALIFY / DEVELOP / NOT QUALIFIED verdict, and a CPD action plan.

Technical & Standard Knowledge
Understanding requirements and their intent
1. Understands the structure and clause hierarchy of the applicable QMS standard(s) — high-level structure, normative references, terms.
ISO/IEC 17021-3 | IAF MD 5 Auditor Competency
2. Can explain the intent (not just wording) of each clause — what outcome the requirement seeks and why.
IAF MD 5 | ISO 19011:2018 §7.2.3
3. Understands sector-specific or regulatory requirements relevant to the audit scope (e.g., AS9100 product safety, IATF CSRs, ISO 13485 design controls, AS9110 RTS authority).
Sector-specific competency per ISO/IEC 17021-3
4. Understands how documented information requirements differ from implementation evidence — can distinguish records from procedures, and both from objective evidence of effectiveness.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4 | Clause 7.5
5. Keeps current with standard revisions, interpretations (SIs), and regulatory amendments in the audit scope; applies current version requirements.
ISO 19011:2018 §7.2.2
2
Audit Methodology
Planning, execution, sampling, evidence gathering
6. Plans audits using risk-based sampling: identifies critical processes, high-risk areas, and prior NC history to allocate time appropriately.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.3 | Clause 9.2.2
7. Uses process-approach auditing: follows the flow of work through inputs, activities, outputs, and interfaces rather than clause-by-clause checklist audit.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.4
8. Selects and applies appropriate sampling methods — judgmental and/or statistical — and can justify sample size relative to population and risk.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.6 | IAF MD 5
9. Applies triangulated evidence gathering — interviews, observation, and document/record review — and can identify when one source is insufficient alone.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.5
10. Distinguishes nonconformities, observations, and opportunities for improvement — applies the correct classification and can articulate the basis for each.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.8 | Clause 10.2
3
NC Writing & Documentation
Defensible findings, evidence citation, report quality
11. Writes nonconformities using a complete structure: requirement cited (by clause), objective evidence observed, statement of nonconformance — no conjecture or recommendations embedded in the NC statement.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.8 | CB audit writing standard
12. Cites evidence specifically — document number, revision, date, record reference, observation, interviewee role — so findings are reproducible and defensible on challenge.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.7
13. Produces audit reports that are complete, factual, and impartial — includes scope, criteria, findings, conclusions, and distribution; delivered on time.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.5
4
Communication & Interview Skills
Questioning, listening, managing interviews, opening/closing
14. Uses open-ended questions effectively to draw out process knowledge; avoids leading, closed, or ambiguous questions during interviews.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.4.c
15. Manages opening and closing meetings professionally: states scope/criteria/method at open; presents findings factually and concisely at close without surprises.
ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.2 / §6.4.9
16. Maintains composure and objectivity when auditees challenge findings or push back; de-escalates without withdrawing valid findings.
ISO 19011:2018 §7.2.2 — personal attributes
5
Process Approach & Risk Thinking
System thinking, risk-based reasoning, context application
17. Understands and applies the process approach: can trace a process from input through transformation, controls, measurement, and output including interfaces with other processes.
ISO 9001:2015 §4.4 | ISO 19011:2018 §6.4.4
18. Applies risk-based thinking: can assess whether the organization's risk and opportunity identification is appropriate and proportionate to its context and objectives.
ISO 9001:2015 §6.1 | ISO 19011:2018 §6.3
19. Audits for effectiveness (does it work?) not just conformance (does it exist?) — assesses whether processes achieve their intended results.
ISO 9001:2015 §9.1 | IAF MD 5 intent-based evaluation
6
Independence, Ethics & Professionalism
Impartiality, confidentiality, audit conduct
20. Declares conflicts of interest before each audit; withdraws from areas where independence cannot be maintained; documents the declaration.
ISO 19011:2018 §7.2.2 | Clause 9.2.2(a)
21. Maintains confidentiality of audit information; does not disclose findings or evidence to unauthorized parties; handles sensitive records appropriately.
ISO 19011:2018 §7.2.2 | Clause 7.5.3
22. Demonstrates professional conduct throughout: punctual, prepared, factual, respectful; does not audit areas they are responsible for; accepts correction of genuine errors without defensiveness.
ISO 19011:2018 §7.2.2 personal attribute requirements
23. Maintains a continuous professional development record — tracks audits completed, training taken, and competency gaps closed over the trailing 3-year period.
ISO 19011:2018 §7.6 | IAF MD 5 maintenance requirements
