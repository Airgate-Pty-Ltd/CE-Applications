Proposes mandatory digital recordkeeping for work orders, inspection records, and authorized release documents under 14 CFR Part 145. Affects all U.S. certificated repair stations. DRS for small repair stations included.

NPA 2026-07: Part-M / Part-CAMO Light Amendment — Continuing Airworthiness

Proposes amendments to Part-M and Part-CAMO to align with ICAO Annex 8 changes and introduce proportionate requirements for ELA2 aircraft. Three-day comment window due to alignment deadline.

EASA AMC/GM Update HIGH Effective: Aug 1, 2026

ED Decision 2026/005/R — SMS Integration AMC/GM: Part-145 Update

Updates AMC/GM to Part-145 to align with MAG Change 10 SMS/SMSVP requirements for U.S.-EU bilateral repair stations. Affects EASA Supplement Section 20 (new SMS section) for Part 145 holders with bilateral approvals.

Consulting flag: Repair stations with bilateral BASA approvals will need Supplement updates. Outreach opportunity for existing MRO clients.

IAQG Sanctioned Interpretation NORMAL

AS9100D SI-006: Configuration Management Records — Acceptable Formats

Clarifies acceptable formats for configuration baseline documentation under clause 8.1.2. Digital records in read-only formats (PDF/A, signed XML) are now explicitly acceptable. Applies immediately to all IAQG-registered CB audits.
