# PRODUCT BRIEF — CMMC 2.0 Level 1 Readiness Self-Assessment

## One-Line Pitch
Every DoD contractor with FCI must pass Level 1. Know where you stand in 45 minutes — before the prime contractor or DCSA does.

## Problem
DFARS 252.204-7019 requires every DoD contractor handling Federal Contract Information (FCI) to conduct an annual CMMC Level 1 self-assessment and submit their score to SPRS. 300,000+ organizations are subject to this requirement. Most lack structured guidance on what the 17 practices actually require in operational terms. A missing or low SPRS score is now visible to prime contractors and can disqualify a supplier from competing for DoD work.

## Solution
A structured, scored self-assessment covering all 17 CMMC Level 1 practices across all 6 required domains (Access Control, Identification & Authentication, Media Protection, Physical Protection, System and Communications Protection, System and Information Integrity). 45 minutes or less. Gap table, domain scoring, and a CMMC Readiness Record for internal documentation.

## Customer
IT Manager, Quality Manager, Compliance Officer, or owner-operator at a DoD contractor or subtier supplier — any organization with a CAGE code that handles FCI.
