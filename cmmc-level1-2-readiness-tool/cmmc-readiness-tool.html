Assessment Setup

Level 1 Assessment: Covers all 17 CMMC Level 1 practices aligned to the 6 Level 1 domains from NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 / FAR 52.204-21. Required for all DoD contractors handling Federal Contract Information (FCI). Self-assessment per 48 CFR 252.204-7019.
Domain 1 — Access Control (AC) AC.L1 — 2 Practices
AC.L1-3.1.1
Limit information system access to authorized users, processes acting on behalf of authorized users, and devices (including other information systems). Access control lists or equivalent mechanisms restrict system access to only those with a defined need. ⚑ Critical
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.1.1; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(i); CMMC L1-AC.1.001
AC.L1-3.1.2
Limit information system access to the types of transactions and functions that authorized users are permitted to execute. Role-based access control or least-privilege principles prevent users from accessing systems or data beyond their authorized function.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.1.2; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(ii); CMMC L1-AC.1.002
Domain 2 — Identification & Authentication (IA) IA.L1 — 2 Practices
IA.L1-3.5.1
Identify information system users, processes acting on behalf of users, and devices. Each user, service account, and device has a unique identifier that is traceable to an individual, process, or device.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.5.1; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iii)
IA.L1-3.5.2
Authenticate (or verify) the identities of users, processes, or devices as a prerequisite to allowing access to organizational information systems. Passwords, PINs, tokens, or certificates are used to authenticate before granting access to systems handling FCI/CUI. ⚑ Critical
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.5.2; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(iv)
Domain 3 — Media Protection (MP) MP.L1 — 1 Practice
MP.L1-3.8.3
Sanitize or destroy information system media before disposal or reuse. Hard drives, USB drives, CDs, and paper containing FCI/CUI are sanitized (wiped/degaussed) or destroyed before disposal. A documented procedure and log of media sanitization exists.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.8.3; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(v); NIST SP 800-88 (media sanitization)
Domain 4 — Physical Protection (PE) PE.L1 — 4 Practices
PE.L1-3.10.1
Limit physical access to organizational information systems to authorized individuals. Server rooms, IT closets, and areas storing FCI/CUI are physically secured with locks, badge access, or equivalent controls restricting entry to authorized personnel only.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.10.1; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(vi)
PE.L1-3.10.3
Escort visitors and monitor visitor activity in areas containing FCI/CUI systems. Visitors are signed in, badged, escorted, and their activities monitored. Access areas with controlled information are not left unmonitored with visitors present.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.10.3; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(vii)
PE.L1-3.10.4
Maintain audit logs of physical access to facilities where FCI/CUI systems reside. Access logs (badge systems, sign-in sheets, camera logs) are retained and reviewed periodically.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.10.4; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(viii)
PE.L1-3.10.5
Control and manage physical access devices (e.g., keys, combinations, access cards, PINs). A current inventory of physical access devices is maintained; devices are retrieved from departing employees; combinations/codes are changed when compromised or upon personnel departure.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.10.5; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(ix)
Domain 5 — System & Communications Protection (SC) SC.L1 — 2 Practices
SC.L1-3.13.1
Monitor, control, and protect organizational communications (i.e., information transmitted or received by organizational information systems) at the external boundaries and key internal boundaries of the information systems. A firewall or equivalent controls communications at the network perimeter.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.13.1; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(x)
SC.L1-3.13.5
Implement subnetworks for publicly accessible system components that are physically or logically separated from internal networks. Publicly accessible servers (web, email) are in a DMZ or logically separated from systems that store or process FCI/CUI.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.13.5; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(xi)
Domain 6 — System & Information Integrity (SI) SI.L1 — 6 Practices
SI.L1-3.14.1
Identify, report, and correct information and information system flaws in a timely manner. A patch management process is in place; vulnerabilities are tracked and remediated according to a defined schedule. Security patches are applied within a defined timeframe.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.14.1; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(xii)
SI.L1-3.14.2
Provide protection from malicious code at appropriate locations within organizational information systems. Antivirus / endpoint protection software is installed and active on all systems processing FCI/CUI. ⚑ Critical
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.14.2; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(xiii)
SI.L1-3.14.4
Update malicious code protection mechanisms when new releases are available. Antivirus / endpoint signatures are updated automatically or on a defined schedule (at minimum daily). Update logs are maintained.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.14.4; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(xiv)
SI.L1-3.14.5
Perform periodic scans of the information system and real-time scans of files from external sources as files are downloaded, opened, or executed. Scheduled system scans occur at minimum weekly; email attachment scanning is active.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.14.5; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(xv)
SI.L1-3.14.6
Monitor organizational information systems to detect attacks and indicators of potential attacks. Network and system monitoring (IDS/IPS, SIEM, or equivalent log review process) is in place to identify anomalous or potentially malicious activity.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.14.6; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(xvi)
SI.L1-3.14.7
Identify unauthorized use of organizational information systems. Baseline of authorized activity / authorized users is documented; deviations from baseline are investigated. Unauthorized system use events are captured in logs.
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 §3.14.7; FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(xvii)

SPRS Self-Assessment Score

DFARS 252.204-7019 requires DoD contractors handling CUI to conduct an annual self-assessment of NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 and upload their score to the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS). The SPRS score is calculated using the 110-practice Level 2 assessment, starting at 110 and deducting points for each unimplemented practice. This tool assesses Level 1 readiness only (17 practices). A SPRS score filing is Brian's action — consult your CAGE code, SPRS portal access, and a qualified assessor for Level 2 / CUI environments.
