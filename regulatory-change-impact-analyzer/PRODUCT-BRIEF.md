# Product Brief — Regulatory Change Impact Analyzer

**Brand:** Conform-Edge | **Route:** Software  
**Status:** Prototype — pending Brian review  
**Date:** 2026-08-25

---

## What It Is
A self-contained HTML tool that converts a regulatory or standards change notice into a structured impact report: which of your certifications are affected, which QMS clause areas are in scope, which document types need updating, and what actions to take — with urgency tiers (IMMEDIATE / SHORT-TERM / MONITOR).

## Target Buyer
Quality Director / VP Quality / Compliance Manager at an AS9100D-, ISO 9001-, IATF 16949-, ISO 13485-, or FAA/EASA Part 145-certified organization.

## Problem Solved
When a regulation or standard changes, quality teams spend hours manually figuring out what it means for their specific QMS. The mapping is inconsistent, incomplete, and done fresh each time. This tool makes it repeatable, structured, and defensible.

## Key Features
- Covers 11 authorities / change types (FAA, EASA, ISO, IAQG, IATF, FDA, CAAC, UK CAA)
- 15 certification/approval scope options
- Urgency tier determination with safety/airworthiness override
- NPRM advisory (proposed rules flagged as not-yet-effective)
- Affected clause families, document types, and action plan
- Management review narrative
- JSON export
