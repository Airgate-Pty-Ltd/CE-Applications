IATF 16949 Planned Change Gate Tool

Cl. 8.1 Cl. 8.2.3.1 Cl. 8.3.6 Deterministic gate for automotive planned change — classification → PPAP trigger → customer notification → authorization

1
Change Classification
Cl. 8.1 / 8.2.3.1
Does this change affect the design, specifications, or approved drawings of a customer-approved product?Critical
Includes tolerance changes, material substitutions, alternative components, dimensional changes.
Does this change affect the manufacturing process, method, sequence, or tooling (including dies, fixtures, gages)?Critical
Includes new or replacement tooling, change of forming/machining/assembly process, sub-process method changes.
Does this change involve a new or different sub-supplier, or a change to the supply source for a controlled material/component?
Includes supplier qualification changes, single-source substitutions, tier-2 sub-supplier changes that affect controlled characteristics.
Does this change involve a new or relocated manufacturing facility, production line, or plant location?
Includes building changes, line transfers, new cell layouts, or outsourcing of previously in-house processes.
2
PPAP Trigger Assessment
AIAG PPAP 4th Ed. / VDA
Do your applicable Customer-Specific Requirements (CSRs) explicitly list this change type as a PPAP trigger?Critical
Check CSRs for GM, Ford, Stellantis, BMW, VW, Toyota, Hyundai, or your specific customers. CSR requirements override PPAP default logic.
Does the change affect any Special Characteristics (SC/CC/KPC) on the approved control plan or drawing?Critical
Special Characteristics include safety, regulatory, and functionally critical features designated by the customer or engineering.
Has your PFMEA been reviewed for this change and updated where risk has increased?
IATF 16949 Cl. 8.3.3.3 requires PFMEA to reflect the current process. Any new failure mode or change to detection/occurrence must be assessed.
Has the Control Plan been updated to reflect the changed process, with revised inspection methods, frequencies, and reaction plans?
Cl. 8.5.1.1 requires control plans to be current. Changes to process or SC impact require documented control plan revision before production.
3
Customer Notification & Approval
Cl. 8.2.3.1 / CSR
Has written notification been sent to the customer engineering or SQE contact for this change?Critical
Cl. 8.2.3.1 requires customer notification before changes to products or processes. Notification must be documented.
If customer approval is required before implementation, has written approval been received?
Some customers (GM, Ford, Stellantis) require explicit engineering approval before production with change. Check your PPAP approval letter / deviation permit requirements.
Does this change require a deviation / production permit while the PPAP is being completed?
IATF Cl. 8.7.1.3 governs customer-authorized deviations. A deviation is time-limited and quantity-limited — never a substitute for PPAP approval.
4
Internal Authorization & Evidence
Cl. 8.1 / 8.5.6
Has the change been reviewed and authorized by the designated internal authority (e.g., engineering, quality, cross-functional team)?
Cl. 8.1 requires controlled implementation of planned changes. Internal authorization must be documented before production launch.
Has pre-production validation (run-at-rate, dimensional buy-off, or capability study) been completed for the changed process?
Pre-launch production validation per Cl. 8.3.4 / PPAP Section 11. Evidence must be available before PPAP submission or production launch.
Has the change been communicated to all affected internal functions (production, quality, logistics, purchasing, maintenance)?
Cl. 8.5.6 change management requires downstream communication. Undocumented communication creates audit vulnerability.
