# PRODUCT BRIEF — ITAR/EAR Export Control Compliance Self-Assessment

## One-Line Pitch
Know your export control exposure before DDTC or BIS does — a structured 45-minute self-assessment for defense manufacturers and their supply chain.

## Problem
ITAR (22 CFR Parts 120–130) and EAR (15 CFR Parts 730–774) impose criminal and civil penalties on defense manufacturers and their subtier suppliers who transfer controlled technology, items, or services without proper licenses or compliance programs. ITAR violations carry up to $1M/violation criminal and $1.3M/violation civil penalties. Yet most SMEs in the defense supply chain have no systematic way to assess their compliance program maturity — law firm audits cost $15,000–$50,000 and are out of reach for the typical subtier supplier.

## Solution
A self-contained, scored self-assessment tool covering the five core pillars of ITAR/EAR compliance: jurisdiction determination, registration and authorization, empowered official and compliance program, deemed export controls, and records management. 19 questions, 5 critical items, 3-tier verdict, 45 minutes or less.

## Customer
Export Compliance Manager, Quality Manager, or Regulatory Affairs Lead at a US aerospace/defense manufacturer, subtier supplier, distributor, research lab, or university technology transfer office with ITAR and/or EAR exposure.
