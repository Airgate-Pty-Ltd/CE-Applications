ISO 42001:2023 Artificial Intelligence Management System Readiness Assessment

This tool helps organizations evaluate their readiness to implement or certify an AI Management System (AIMS) aligned to ISO 42001:2023. Answer all 30 questions to generate a scored gap report, priority action list, and management review narrative. Items marked CRITICAL carry an automatic override — any critical item unanswered, partial, or not implemented results in a NOT READY verdict regardless of overall score.
