## Notes for the author

- **Section 5's physical-environment cluster (5.6-5.9, plus 5.11)** is the single largest concentration of gaps — 5 of 18. This library was written as cognitive/behavioral HF training; none of it touches industrial-hygiene-style physical-environment content. If Annex Quality has (or plans) a separate ergonomics/industrial-hygiene course, that may be a faster path to closing these than authoring net-new HF-specific segments.
- **Three items (4.13, 6.4, 9.1)** had real, seemingly-usable candidate content before the cross-jurisdiction ruling — all three were CCAR-145 R4-cited (AQ-160) or purpose-mismatched (DOT program for 4.13) and are excluded per the owner's decision that CCAR-145 citations must not appear in the GM1/EASA traceability chain. If Annex Quality has a framework-neutral equivalent, or is willing to author a citation-free version of the same underlying content, that closes these three efficiently since the subject-matter expertise already exists in-house.
- **Section 1a (the four SMS-pillar items) is not in this list** — it's now covered via AQ-080 (Safety Risk Management) and AQ-081 (Safety Assurance), per the owner's ruling bringing it in scope. See the import execution log for exact sourcing.
