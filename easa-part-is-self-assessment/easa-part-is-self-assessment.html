EASA Part-IS (Information Security) Compliance Self-Assessment

This tool provides aviation organizations subject to EASA Part-IS (Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/1645 and 2023/203) with a structured compliance self-assessment. Complete all 28 questions to generate a scored gap report, management review narrative, and JSON record. Items marked CRITICAL carry automatic override — any critical item unanswered or partial results in a NOT READY verdict.
