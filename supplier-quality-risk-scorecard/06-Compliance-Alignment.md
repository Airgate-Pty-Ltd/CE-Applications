## Purpose
This document maps Supplier Quality Risk Scorecard features to regulatory and standards requirements. It proves that the tool helps organizations achieve and maintain compliance with aerospace, automotive, medical device, and general quality standards.

## AS9100D (Aerospace Quality Management System) — Rev D

### Relevant Clauses
**8.4 Control of Externally Provided Processes, Products and Services**

**Requirement:**
Organizations must establish and implement processes to ensure that externally provided products and services conform to requirements. This includes oversight of:
- Supplier capability (quality system, certifications)
- Product / service acceptance criteria
- Change management
- Verification of outsourced processes

**How SQ Risk Scorecard Helps:**
- ✅ **Supplier Approval:** Risk scorecard identifies suppliers meeting baseline capability (certification status + audit history)
- ✅ **Ongoing Monitoring:** Capability score tracks supplier performance over time
- ✅ **Audit Planning:** Auto-generated SQA checklist ensures consistent oversight of critical suppliers
- ✅ **Evidence:** Scorecard + audit history provides audit trail for regulatory proof
- ✅ **Counterfeit Parts:** AS9100D-specific SQA includes counterfeit parts prevention flow-down

**Audit Question for Auditor:**
*"Can you demonstrate that your key suppliers maintain acceptable quality systems and that you have current audit evidence?" → Answer: SQ Risk Scorecard shows supplier risk, audit dates, certification status.*

## AS9110C (Aerospace Maintenance, Repair, Organization QMS) — Rev C

### Relevant Clauses
**8.4 Supplier Control + 8.6.2 Return to Service (RTS)**

**Requirement:**
MRO / repair organizations must:
- Verify supplier capability before authorizing repair work
- Ensure suppliers are approved / qualified
- Document supplier audits and surveillance
- Verify suppliers use approved repair specifications

**How SQ Risk Scorecard Helps:**
- ✅ **Supplier Approval Tracking:** Dashboard shows which suppliers hold required certifications (EASA Part-145, FAA Part-145, CAAC CCAR-145)
- ✅ **Audit Due Dates:** Auto-alerts when supplier audit is overdue
- ✅ **Flow-Down Compliance:** SQA generator ensures return-to-service requirements flowed down
- ✅ **Compliance Evidence:** Scorecard proves to FAA/EASA inspector that supplier oversight is current

**Use Case:**
*Before authorizing a repair, MRO manager checks supplier scorecard. If supplier is "Red," manager requires updated audit before work begins.*

## FAA Part 21 & Part 145 (US Production Approval & Repair Station)

### Relevant Regulations
**14 CFR Part 21.309 (Subpart E)** — Supplier Approval for PMA holders
**14 CFR Part 145.209** — Repair station supplier control

**Requirement:**
- Suppliers must be FAA-approved or operate under equivalent QMS
- Organizations must audit suppliers and document approvals
- Configuration management and traceability required

**How SQ Risk Scorecard Helps:**
- ✅ **Supplier Approval Status:** Scorecard tracks FAA approval vs. alternative QMS (AS9100, ISO 9001)
- ✅ **Audit Schedule:** Ensures mandatory supplier audits occur at required intervals (typically 24 months)
- ✅ **SQA Flow-Down:** Auto-generated checklist includes FAA-specific requirements (JSARA, product safety, traceability)
- ✅ **Audit Evidence:** CSV export provides auditable record of supplier oversight for FAA inspection

**FAA Inspector Question:**
*"Show me your supplier audit plan and the last audit results for [supplier]." → Answer: SQ Risk Scorecard shows audit dates, scope, and certification status.*

## EASA Part 145 & MAG Change 10 (EU Repair Station)

### Relevant Regulations
**EASA Part 145.501** — Supplier Control
**MAG CHG 10 Section 18** — Supplier requirements (effective 2026)

**Requirement:**
- EASA repair stations must audit suppliers and maintain SQAs
- Suppliers must be approved / monitored continuously
- EASA Form 8 audits must verify supplier oversight is compliant
- Section 20 (new): SMS must include supplier safety risk management

**How SQ Risk Scorecard Helps:**
- ✅ **Supplier Risk Assessment:** 4-dimension scoring aligns with EASA Section 20 SMS supplier risk requirements
- ✅ **SQA Documentation:** Auto-generated checklist meets EASA Part 145.501(c) requirements
- ✅ **Audit Trail:** Scorecard provides 7-year-retention evidence for EASA inspector
- ✅ **SMS Integration:** Risk matrix supports SMS supplier safety risk identification

**EASA Form 8 Audit Question:**
*"How do you identify and manage supplier safety risks?" → Answer: SQ Risk Scorecard dashboard shows supplier risk levels, audit schedule, and corrective actions.*

## CAAC CCAR-145 (Chinese Repair Station) — Effective 2025–2026

### Relevant Regulations
**CCAR-145.501** — Supplier Control (newly aligned with EASA)

**Requirement:**
- Chinese repair stations must document supplier assessment
- Suppliers must have acceptable QMS (certification or audit evidence)
- MOU (Memorandum of Understanding) between repair station and supplier required
- Annual audit schedule documented

**How SQ Risk Scorecard Helps:**
- ✅ **Supplier Assessment:** Risk scorecard proves supplier capability assessment to CAAC
- ✅ **MOU Documentation:** SQA checklist serves as basis for MOU content
- ✅ **Audit Schedule:** Scorecard auto-tracks audit dates, provides audit planning evidence
- ✅ **Compliance Proof:** Dashboard + audit history demonstrates ongoing supplier control

**CAAC Inspector Question:**
*"Show your supplier assessment and audit schedule." → Answer: Export SQ Risk Scorecard to PDF for presentation.*

## IATF 16949:2016 (Automotive Supplier Quality)

### Relevant Clauses
**8.4 Control of Externally Provided Processes, Products and Services**

**Requirement:**
- Tier-1 suppliers must audit Tier-2 suppliers (PPAP, APQP, FMEA, SPC evidence)
- Supplier approval includes process capability (Cpk/Ppk) verification
- Regular supplier performance reviews and scorecards

**How SQ Risk Scorecard Helps:**
- ✅ **Supplier Scorecard:** Tool provides required supplier performance dashboard for Tier-1 QBR (Quarterly Business Review)
- ✅ **Capability Tracking:** Process Capability dimension maps to Cpk requirements
- ✅ **Audit Schedule:** Tier-1 supply chain audits (mandatory every 24 months) auto-tracked
- ✅ **PPAP Readiness:** SQA generator includes PPAP first-article evidence checklist

**OEM Audit Requirement:**
*"Show supplier performance scorecard and audit schedule." → Answer: SQ Risk Scorecard exports executive-ready dashboard.*

## ISO 13485:2016 & FDA QMSR (Medical Device QMS)

### Relevant Clauses
**ISO 13485:8.4 & FDA 21 CFR Part 820.50** — Supplier Evaluation & Selection

**Requirement:**
- Suppliers (components, services, testing) must be evaluated and approved
- Supplier agreements must define requirements (CoC, traceability, quality system)
- Design History File (DHF) must document supplier selection and approval
- FDA inspection expects supplier QMS evidence

**How SQ Risk Scorecard Helps:**
- ✅ **Supplier Qualification:** Risk scorecard documents ISO 13485 certification / equivalent QMS
- ✅ **SQA as Control Document:** Generated SQA checklist becomes supplier agreement
- ✅ **DHF Evidence:** Export scorecard + SQA = evidence supplier was evaluated per 21 CFR 820.50
- ✅ **Traceability:** CSV export tracks supplier certification status over product lifecycle
- ✅ **FDA Readiness:** Tool helps prepare for FDA inspection of supplier control section

**FDA Inspector Question:**
*"Show me how you evaluated your key suppliers and verified their quality systems." → Answer: SQ Risk Scorecard + SQA + audit history.*
