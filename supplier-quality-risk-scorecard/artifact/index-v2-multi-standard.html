Risk Scoring Model

Each supplier receives a score 0-100 across 4 equally-weighted dimensions:

  • Certification Status (25%): Current = 5pts, Soon = 3pts, Expired = 1pt, Not Required = 0pts
  • Process Capability (25%): Your rating 1-5
  • Audit History (25%): Recent = 5pts, < 1 year = 3pts, > 1 year = 1pt
  • Compliance Gap (25%): No NCRs = 5pts, 1-2 NCRs = 3pts, 3+ NCRs = 1pt

Risk Levels: Green 75-100 (Low) | Yellow 50-74 (Medium) | Orange 25-49 (High) | Red 0-24 (Critical)

Standards & Content Packs

SQA checklists are generated from data-driven content packs aligned to each standard. Each standard pack includes requirements for:

  • Sourcing Control: Supplier approval, qualification, certification
  • Inspection & Acceptance: Receiving, test reports, traceability
  • Flow-Down Requirements: How [The Organization] requirements flow to supplier
  • Corrective Action & Audit: Nonconformance, SCAR, audit schedule

Data & Privacy

All supplier data is stored locally in your browser. No data is sent to external servers. To migrate to a new browser or device, use CSV Export to back up your data.
