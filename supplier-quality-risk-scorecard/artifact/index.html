Help & Documentation

Quick Start

1. View Dashboard: Click Dashboard to see all suppliers color-coded by risk (Green/Yellow/Orange/Red).

2. Add Supplier: Use Add Supplier form to enter supplier data. All fields are auto-scored.

3. Generate SQA: Use SQA Generator to create an audit checklist for any supplier in your target standard.

4. Export: Use Import/Export to backup your data or integrate with other systems.

Risk Scoring

Suppliers are scored 0-100 across 4 dimensions:

  • Certification Status (25%): Current = 5pts, Expiring Soon = 3pts, Expired = 1pt
  • Process Capability (25%): Manual rating 1-5
  • Audit History (25%): Recent audit = 5pts, < 1 year = 3pts, > 1 year = 1pt
  • Compliance Gap (25%): No NCRs = 5pts, 1-2 NCRs = 3pts, 3+ NCRs = 1pt

Risk Levels: Green (75-100) = Low Risk, Yellow (50-74) = Medium, Orange (25-49) = High, Red (0-24) = Critical

Data Persistence

All supplier data is stored in your browser's local storage. To back up, use the CSV Export feature. To reset to demo data, click "Reset Demo" in the menu.
