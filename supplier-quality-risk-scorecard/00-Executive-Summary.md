# Supplier Quality Risk Scorecard & Flow-Down Compliance Tracker
## Executive Summary

**Opportunity:** Supplier Quality Risk Scorecard & Flow-Down Compliance Tracker — SaaS tool for quality managers to assess supplier risk across AS9100/ISO 9001/IATF/ISO 13485 standards and automatically generate flow-down compliance checklists.

**Problem:** Organizations managing suppliers across aerospace, automotive, and medical device face 40–60% manual effort in:
- Assessing supplier capability against changing standards
- Tracking which supplier holds which certifications and approvals
- Creating/updating supplier quality agreements (SQAs)
- Generating audit-ready compliance matrices
- Identifying repeat low-performers before they cause field failures

**Revenue Model:** $199–$999/month (tiered by # suppliers + integrations); projected $380k–$520k Year 1 conservative; $1.2M+ Year 3 with premium features.

**Why Now:** FAA Part 21/145 supplier audits increasing post-COVID; IATF 16949 tier-1 suppliers under heightened OEM pressure; ISO 13485 design history file compliance now FDA-scrutinized; automotive supply chain chaos driving stricter controls.

**Why Brian:** 20 years aerospace/auto/medical QMS audits → knows the real supplier pain. Master-investigation-os + audit skills (AS9100D, ISO 9001, IATF 16949, ISO 13485) + Conform-Edge training = unique positioning. No direct competitor with this integration depth.

**MVP Deliverable:**
- Self-contained HTML app: 4-dimension supplier risk scorecard (Certification Status 25%, Process Capability 25%, Audit History 25%, Compliance Gap 25%)
- Auto-generated SQA checklist (governance-customizable by standard)
- CSV import + demo data + export-to-PDF
- Zero backend required; runs offline in browser
