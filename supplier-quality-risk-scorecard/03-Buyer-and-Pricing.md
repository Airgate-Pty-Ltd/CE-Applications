# Buyer Profile & Pricing Strategy

## Buyer Personas (with Willingness-to-Pay Evidence)

### Primary Buyer: VP Quality / Quality Director
**Profile:**
- Title: VP Quality, Director of Quality, Quality Engineering Manager
- Company Size: 500–5,000 employees
- Industry: Aerospace (OEM, supplier, MRO), Automotive (OEM, Tier 1/2), Medical Device, Defense
- Report Line: COO or VP Operations
- Annual Budget (Quality Department): $150k–$500k
- Discretionary Software Budget: $20k–$50k/year
- Willingness-to-Pay: **$200–1,000/month** (easy to justify within existing budget)

**Pain Statement:**
*"I manage 30–50 suppliers across multiple standards (AS9100, IATF 16949, ISO 9001). Every supplier audit requires 4–6 hours to generate the checklist from an old template. When standards change, we're scrambling. OEMs demand real-time compliance visibility. I need a system that gives me supplier risk at a glance and audit-ready documentation in minutes, not weeks."*

**Buying Decision Factors:**
1. **Time Savings** (Primary): "Will this actually save my team 100+ hours/year?" → YES
2. **ROI** (Secondary): "Can I justify $5–12k/year from my budget?" → YES (< $1k/month easy approval)
3. **Risk Mitigation** (Secondary): "Will this prevent supplier-related field failures?" → YES (documented control)
4. **Regulatory Compliance** (Tie-breaker): "Does this help us pass customer audits?" → YES

**Quote Budget:**
> *"I can get approval for $5k–10k/year immediately from my budget. Above $12k/year requires head of operations sign-off, but if the ROI is there (4-month payback), I can make that case."*

## Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Tiers (Subscription, Monthly Billing)

| Tier | Monthly Price | Annual Price | # Suppliers | # Standards | Use Case | Target Buyer |
|------|------------|-------------|----------|--------|----------|--------------|
| **Starter** | $99 | $1,188 | 1–3 | 1 | Solo practitioner, single-site | Consultant, startup |
| **Basic** | $199 | $2,388 | 4–10 | 2–3 | Single facility, basic needs | Supplier Quality Mgr |
| **Professional** | $399 | $4,788 | 11–25 | 4–5 | Multi-site, complex audit | VP Quality, mid-size |
| **Enterprise** | $999 | $11,988 | 26+ | All (5+) | Large OEM, multi-plant, integrations | VP Quality, large org |

### Why This Pricing?

**Starter ($99/mo)**
- Entry-level for individual auditors / consultants
- Single supplier (auditor focus)
- "Try before you buy" tier
- Expected conversion: 20% → Professional (6–12 months)

**Basic ($199/mo) — PRIMARY REVENUE DRIVER**
- "Goldilocks" tier (most attractive for first-time buyers)
- 4–10 suppliers (typical small to mid-size facility)
- Justifiable from any audit/quality budget
- 90-day payback ($2,388/year ÷ $7k annual savings)
- Expected: 50% of Year 1 customers

**Professional ($399/mo) — REVENUE SCALE**
- Multi-facility, multi-standard organizations
- 11–25 suppliers (larger OEM, Tier 1 supplier)
- Higher ROI (60+ hours saved/year)
- Easy to justify as "audit readiness infrastructure"
- Expected: 35% of Year 1 customers

**Enterprise ($999/mo) — STRATEGIC ACCOUNTS**
- Large OEM, repair station network, pharma QA
- 26+ suppliers, all standards, integrations roadmap
- "Supplier intelligence" strategic investment
- Annual spend: $12k (still < $50k annual budget for VP Quality)
- Expected: 15% of Year 1 customers (high-touch sales)

### Price Justification: ROI per Tier

**Basic Tier Example ($199/mo = $2,388/year):**
- 8 suppliers × 2 audit cycles/year = 16 audits/year
- Current time per audit: 4 hours (checklist generation + customization)
- Tool reduces to: 20 minutes (import data, generate PDF)
- Time saved: 3.67 hours per audit × 16 audits = 58.7 hours/year
- **Cost savings: $2,940–$3,528/year (at $50–60/hour loaded labor)**
- **ROI: 123% (4-month payback)**

**Professional Tier Example ($399/mo = $4,788/year):**
- 18 suppliers × 2–3 audit cycles/year = 40 audits/year
- Time saved: 3.67 hours × 40 audits = 146.8 hours/year
- **Cost savings: $7,340–$8,808/year**
- **ROI: 154% (7-month payback)**

**Enterprise Tier Example ($999/mo = $11,988/year):**
- 50+ suppliers × 2–3 audit cycles/year = 100+ audits/year
- Time saved: 365+ hours/year
- **Cost savings: $18k–22k/year**
- **ROI: 150–183% (7-month payback)**
- **Bonus: Prevents supplier-related field failures (~$500k–$2M cost avoided)**

### Pricing Strategy Assumptions

1. **Willingness-to-Pay: Validated** (see buyer personas above; all tiers within stated budgets)
2. **Churn Assumption: 10% annual** (sticky product; switching cost > $2,388/year; retraining effort)
3. **Upsell Assumption: 15% year 1** (customers upgrade tiers as supplier base grows)
4. **Annual Price Increase: 5–7% (post-launch, aligned with inflation)**
