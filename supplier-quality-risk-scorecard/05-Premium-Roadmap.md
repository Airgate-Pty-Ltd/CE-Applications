# Premium Roadmap: v1.1 through v3.0+

## v1.1 (Month 2–3 Post-Launch) — User-Requested Features

### Features
- **Cloud Backup (Opt-In)** — Encrypted cloud storage for supplier data (AWS S3); user-controlled sync
- **Supplier Notes** — Free-form text field per supplier for audit observations, follow-up items
- **Audit History Timeline** — Track supplier score changes over time; show trend (improving/declining)
- **Email Alerts** — Automated reminders when supplier audit is due or certification expires
- **Multi-User Roles** — Read-only auditor view, edit access for manager, admin access for owner

### Revenue Impact
- Cloud storage add-on: +$10/mo per customer (30% adoption = +$600 MRR from 100 customers)
- Email alerts: included in base subscription

### Development Effort
- 40 hours (cloud storage + email service integration)

### Launch: October 2026

---

## v2.0 (Month 4–6 Post-Launch) — AI & Integrations

### Features
- **AI-Assisted Risk Scoring** — Machine learning predicts supplier risk from historical audit data
  - Learns pattern: past NCRs + capability = future risk
  - Identifies at-risk suppliers before audit
  - Confidence interval (80%, 90%, 95% models)
- **Supplier Portal** — Self-service: suppliers upload certs, test reports, audit responses
  - Reduces back-and-forth emails
  - Speeds up audit readiness
  - Compliance evidence submission simplified
- **Integration API** — Connect to ERP, QMS, audit tools
  - Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, SAP integrations
  - Auto-sync supplier data
  - Two-way sync: audit results → ERP
- **Compliance Alert Engine** — Automated notifications when standards change
  - Monitor ISO, IATF, FAA regulatory updates
  - Alerts for customer-specific requirement changes
  - Compliance gap re-scoring (auto-updates supplier risk)
- **Advanced Analytics** — Supplier benchmarking, KPI dashboard, predictive maintenance
  - "Compare my suppliers to industry average"
  - Churn prediction (identify suppliers likely to lose certification)
  - Field failure risk scoring

### Pricing (Add-Ons)
- AI Risk Scoring: +$50/mo (included in Professional/Enterprise)
- Supplier Portal: +$100/mo (included in Enterprise)
- Integration API: +$200/mo (Enterprise only)
- Compliance Alert Engine: +$30/mo (included in Professional/Enterprise)

### Revenue Impact
- v2.0 upsell to existing Basic customers → 40% upgrade to Professional (+$10k MRR)
- Integration API for large customers (+$2k MRR)
- Compliance alerts sticky feature (+$1k MRR)
- **Total v2.0 Year 1 revenue lift: +$13k–$20k MRR**

### Development Effort
- 120 hours (AI model training, portal backend, API, alerts)

### Launch: December 2026

### v2.0 Revenue Model Example (100 customers)
- Basic tier (40 customers @ $199): $7,960/mo
- Professional tier (50 customers @ $399 + AI+Alerts): $20,450/mo (w/ add-ons)
- Enterprise tier (10 customers @ $999 + full suite): $11,190/mo
- **Total: $39.6k MRR ($475k annualized)**

## v3.0 (Month 7–12 Post-Launch) — Multi-Org & Consulting Model

### Features
- **Multi-Organization Support** — One account manages suppliers for multiple company entities
  - Consultants audit many clients → white-label by client
  - Enterprises with multiple facilities → shared supplier database
  - Supply chain consortiums → shared compliance visibility
- **Custom Scoring Models** — Build org-specific supplier risk rubrics
  - Weight dimensions per org needs (some prioritize cost, others quality)
  - Custom standards (client-specific requirements added to SQA)
  - Compliance thresholds configurable
- **Advanced Reporting Suite** — Executive dashboards, CAPA tracking, trend analysis
  - Monthly supplier scorecard (board-ready PDF)
  - Supplier performance KPI dashboard
  - Trend analysis: "Risk increasing over 6 months → trigger review"
  - Compliance audit trail (prove to regulators you're monitoring)
- **Certification Readiness Assessment** — Auto-audit supplier QMS against multiple standards simultaneously
  - "Is this supplier FAA Part 21 + EASA Part-145 + CAAC CCAR-145 ready?"
  - Multi-standard compliance scoring
  - Certification roadmap (what to fix to achieve certification)
- **Consulting Services Marketplace** — Recommend audit partners, quality consultants
  - Affiliate revenue: refer auditors/consultants → get commission
  - Pre-vetted auditors for supplier audits
  - Marketplace credibility: "Annex Quality recommended auditor"

### Pricing (Premium Tier)
- Multi-Organization: +$500/mo (Consulting/Large Account tier)
- Custom Scoring Models: +$200/mo
- Advanced Reporting Suite: +$150/mo (included in Enterprise+)
- Certification Readiness: +$100/mo (included in Enterprise+)
- Marketplace: 10% commission on referrals (no upfront cost)

### Revenue Impact
- Multi-org consulting accounts: 3–5 customers @ $2k–$5k/mo = +$12k/mo
- Custom scoring: 20% of Professional customers upgrade = +$4k/mo
- Advanced reporting: sticky feature, 100% retention = +$2k/mo
- Marketplace commissions: 5% of referred audit spend = +$5k–$10k/mo (as ecosystem grows)
- **Total v3.0 Year 1 revenue lift: +$23k–$30k MRR**

### Development Effort
- 150 hours (multi-org infrastructure, custom scoring config UI, reporting, marketplace)

### Launch: June 2027

### v3.0 Revenue Model Example (200 customers)
- Starter tier (30 @ $99): $2,970/mo
- Basic tier (80 @ $199): $15,920/mo
- Professional tier (70 @ $499 w/ add-ons): $34,930/mo
- Enterprise tier (15 @ $1,999 w/ full suite): $29,985/mo
- Consulting/Marketplace tier (5 @ $3,500): $17,500/mo

## Long-Term Vision (v4.0+, Year 2–3)

### Possible Features (Research Phase)
- **AI Supplier Intelligence** — Predictive maintenance for supplier relationships
  - "Supplier X likely to lose certification in 3 months; prepare backup supplier"
  - Supply chain risk visualization: single-source suppliers, geographic concentration
  - Supplier financial health integration (identify insolvency risk)
- **Sustainability & ESG Scoring** — Extend supplier risk to ESG compliance
  - Supplier environmental certifications (ISO 14001, carbon footprint)
  - Social responsibility compliance (labor practices, anti-corruption)
  - Board-ready ESG supplier dashboard
- **Integration with OEM Systems** — Connect to Boeing, Airbus, OEM portals
  - Auto-submit supplier data to customer QMS
  - Receive customer audit schedules / requirements
  - Real-time compliance status visibility for customer
- **Blockchain Supply Chain Traceability** — Immutable audit trail for regulators
  - Supplier audit evidence on blockchain (tamper-proof)
  - Traceability from raw material → finished product
  - Regulatory proof-of-compliance (FDA, FAA, EASA)

### Revenue Vision
- v4.0+ positioning: "Premium" tier at $5k–$10k/mo (large OEMs, pharma, defense)
- Year 3 projected revenue: **$2M–$5M annualized** (500–1k customers)

## Customer Success Metrics (Drive Upsell)

**Trigger v2.0 upgrade when:**
- Customer reports > 50 hours/year saved (AI scoring can save additional 30 hours/year)
- Customer has > 5 suppliers and wants predictive insights

**Trigger v3.0 upgrade when:**
- Customer manages > 2 company facilities (multi-org needed)
- Customer is a consulting firm (white-label + marketplace)
- Customer requests custom scoring model
