## Problem Statement

FAA Part 145 repair station training records are one of the most-cited deficiency areas in FAA FSDO inspections and AS9110 audits. Common findings: training records missing for job functions performed, initial training not completed before a technician was assigned, recurrent training overdue, OJT sign-offs missing, supervisory competence assessments not documented, and training program not reflecting actual current scope of work.

The training record requirement is straightforward on its face (§145.163) but the evidentiary standard is higher in practice — inspectors audit individual technician records against their actual job assignments. Repair stations frequently discover gaps only during the inspection itself.

No self-audit tool specifically addresses the §145.163 / AC 145-10 training records standard at the individual-technician level.

---

## Product Definition

**What it does:** A self-contained HTML audit tool that (1) walks the Quality Manager through the training program compliance requirements (training program elements per AC 145-10), and (2) provides a technician-level records gap worksheet where the assessor can evaluate individual training record completeness against job assignment. Outputs a prioritized finding report and management review narrative.

**Who uses it:** Quality Managers, Training Managers, and Compliance Officers at FAA Part 145 repair stations; also usable by AS9110 lead auditors as a pre-audit checklist.

**Why it eliminates work:** Replaces ad-hoc record review with a structured, regulation-specific audit trail. The dual-mode design (program compliance + technician-level spot-check) mirrors how FAA inspectors actually conduct training record audits — most tools only address the program level.

**FSA Section 12:** PASS — same rationale as EASA Form 8. This is a qualitative audit tool, not a data-import problem. The assessor's expert judgment on record completeness is the input; the tool structures that judgment into a defensible finding report.
