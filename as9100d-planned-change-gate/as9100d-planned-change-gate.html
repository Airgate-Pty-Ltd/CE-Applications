AS9100D Planned Change Gate Tool

AS9100D Cl. 8.1.2 Change Management Determine the approval pathway for a planned change and generate the required evidence checklist and notification determination.

Purpose: AS9100D Clause 8.1.2 requires organizations to plan, implement, control, review, and approve changes to operational planning to ensure continued conformity of products and services. This tool applies a deterministic gate to determine whether a proposed change may PROCEED, requires a HOLD for further review, or must be ESCALATED before implementation.
