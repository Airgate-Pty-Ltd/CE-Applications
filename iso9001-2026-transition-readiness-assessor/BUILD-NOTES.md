# Build Notes — iso9001-2026-gap-analyzer

## What was built

**`iso9001-2026-transition-readiness-assessor.html`** — A fully self-contained, client-side HTML assessment tool (no backend, no dependencies). Users answer 20 questions across 5 categories, then click "Generate Report" to receive:
- Overall transition readiness score (0–100%) with a visual ring chart
- Per-section scores rendered as bar charts
- Prioritized action plan sorted by gap severity (High / Partial)
- Print-to-PDF capability for deliverable-ready output
- Conform-Edge branding (navy/steel/gold palette, Aptos-equivalent font stack)

**Sections covered:**
1. Organizational Context & Climate Change (Clauses 4.1–4.2) — 4 questions
2. Leadership, Quality Culture & Ethical Behavior (Clause 5) — 4 questions
3. Risk Planning & Digitalization Strategy (Clause 6) — 4 questions
4. Digital Competence & Documented Information (Clause 7) — 4 questions
5. Performance Monitoring & Improvement (Clauses 9–10) — 4 questions

**Total: 20 questions, 100 points max, 4 scoring tiers:**
- 85–100% = Low Risk
- 65–84% = Moderate Risk
- 40–64% = High Risk
- <40% = Critical Risk

## Why this pick

**ISO 9001:2026 Early-Adopter Gap Analyzer** was ranked #1 of 5 candidates because:

1. **Market size**: ISO 9001 is the world's most-used management system standard. The transition affects every certified organization globally — an order of magnitude larger than medical device (QMSR) or aerospace (AS9100) markets.
2. **Timing window**: FDIS published, September 2026 publication confirmed. This tool lands 1–3 months before the standard drops — maximum first-mover value. By November 2026 the market will be flooded.
3. **Automation**: 100% digital. No ongoing Brian involvement per sale. Sell once, deliver instantly.
4. **Brian's edge**: Deep ISO 9001 expertise + CB auditor background means the questions and actions are authoritative, not generic. That's the moat competitors (bloggers writing "10 things to know about ISO 9001:2026") cannot match.
5. **QMSR** was a close second (confirmed Feb 2026 active compliance crisis, warning letters flying) but the market is 20× smaller than ISO 9001. QMSR is the right pick for next session.

## Key assumptions

1. **[ASSUMPTION]** ISO 9001:2026 Clause 4.1 requires explicit climate change consideration — confirmed by FDIS reporting and the 2024 Climate Amendment that preceded it. Specific clause text not yet final.
2. **[ASSUMPTION]** Clause 5.1.1 explicitly names "quality culture" and "ethical behavior" — confirmed by multiple CB and registrar analyses of the FDIS (DQS, SGS, BSI, Intertek).
3. **[ASSUMPTION]** Digitalization is addressed in Clause 7 (competence/documented information) with explicit digital strategy expectation — confirmed by Ideagen and PSC Software FDIS analyses.
4. **[ASSUMPTION]** Three-year transition window (~Sept 2029) — consistent across all CB sources. Final transition dates not set until standard publishes.
5. **[ASSUMPTION]** The tool does NOT reproduce actual FDIS clause text (copyright issue). It paraphrases intent based on public CB guidance. Brian should verify against the published standard before marketing as "standard-aligned."
6. **[ASSUMPTION]** Annex A (15 pages, new) will clarify the structure — this tool's question framing is consistent with that intent but may need updating once Annex A text is public.
