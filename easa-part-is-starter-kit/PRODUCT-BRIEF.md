# Product Brief — easa-part-is-starter-kit

## Product Name
EASA Part-IS Information Security Compliance Starter Kit

## Product Category
QMS Kit / Consulting Accelerator (Annex Quality hard-copy route)

## Buyer
Quality Managers, Compliance Managers, and Accountable Managers at EASA-regulated
organizations: Part-145 maintenance organizations, Part-21 design/production
organizations, CAMOs — especially US repair stations with EASA bilateral approval
that have no dedicated information-security function.

## Pain
Part-IS (Regulation (EU) 2022/1645 for Part-21/748-2012 organizations, applicable
October 2025; Regulation (EU) 2023/203 for Part-145/CAMO/1321-2014 organizations,
applicable February 2026) requires an Information Security Management System
(ISMS) integrated with the existing management system. Most small/mid AMOs have
no ISMS, no ISO 27001 experience, and no idea what "proportionate" compliance
looks like. Competent authorities are now surveilling against it. [ASSUMPTION:
enforcement posture in 2026 surveillance cycles — verify with current NAA/EASA
communications before selling.]

## Decision Supported
"Are we Part-IS compliant, what are our gaps, and what is the minimum
proportionate ISMS we must stand up before our next audit?"

## Data Required
Organization scope/approvals, interfaces with other regulated orgs, existing QMS/
SMS structure, IT asset inventory, current security controls, outsourcing map.

## Evidence Produced
Gap assessment record, ISMS manual section, risk assessment register, incident
reporting procedure records, training records, management review inputs —
the artifact set an authority audit will sample.

## Recommendation Logic
Requirement-by-requirement gap matrix (intent-based) → each gap mapped to a
remediation action, an owner, and the evidence that closes it. Checklist scoring
tiers: compliant / partial / missing → prioritized by audit exposure.

## Business Outcome
Audit readiness for the first Part-IS surveillance cycle; avoids findings and
approval risk; converts a new regulation into a bounded 60–90 day project.

## Why This Is Different
Not an ISO 27001 template dump. Scoped to Part-IS's proportionate, aviation-
specific requirements, integrated with the QMS/SMS the buyer already runs, and
written by an auditor who audits these organizations. Ledger check: no prior
Part-IS product exists in the portfolio (regulatory-intel-aerospace tracks the
regulation; nothing productized it).

## Pricing Hypothesis
$499 kit (self-serve). 500 sales/yr across EASA-approved orgs (thousands of
Part-145/CAMO/Part-21 holders + US bilateral stations) = $249,500/yr.
Upsell: implementation support / Annex Quality training module.

## First-Dollar Path
Direct outreach to US repair stations with EASA approval (Brian's existing
network + bilateral audit contacts); Gumroad/landing page; LinkedIn post on the
February 2026 applicability date.

## Reuse / IP Captured
Part-IS requirement decomposition (reusable as a Conform-Edge auditor skill and
a future regulatory-intel product); ISMS-integrated-with-QMS pattern reusable for
FAA Part 5 SMS and ISO 27001 crosswalks.
