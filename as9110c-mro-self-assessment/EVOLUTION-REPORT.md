## PRODUCT DIFFERENTIATION

### vs. AS9100D
**AS9100D** = Production/manufacturing focus (design control, special processes, counterfeit prevention).  
**AS9110C** = Maintenance/repair focus (return-to-service, certifying staff, airworthiness release, shift handover).

**Key MRO-specific clauses AS9110C adds:**
- **5.4.1.1** — Return-to-Service (RTS) authority structure
- **5.4.1.2** — Shift Handover procedures (critical for round-the-clock operations)
- **8.2.3** — Airworthiness Release (statement of compliance)
- **8.5.3.2** — Configuration Management (track aircraft configurations)

### Market Position
**Identical value proposition to AS9100D, but:**
- **Narrower market:** FAA Part 145 repair stations only (vs. thousands of aerospace suppliers)
- **Higher pain:** RTS compliance audits are expensive; this tool saves $5k–$10k per company
- **More urgent:** MRO operators face immediate pressures (turnaround times, labor costs)
