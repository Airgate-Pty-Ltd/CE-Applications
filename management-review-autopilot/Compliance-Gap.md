# Compliance Expansion — Management Review Autopilot

## Native today
- ISO 9001:2015 (clause 9.3, 12 inputs)
- AS9100D (clause 9.3, 14 inputs — adds OTD performance + aerospace customer-conformity input)
- AS9110C (clause 9.3, 15 inputs — adds OTD, safety objectives/human factors, airworthiness status)
- ISO 13485:2016 (clause 5.6, 12 inputs — own structure, not a relabeled ISO 9001 pack)

## Commercially valuable expansion, ranked

| Standard | Clause | Why it's valuable | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISO 14001:2015 | 9.3 | Shares 9.3 structure closely with ISO 9001 (HLS-aligned) — near-copy of BASE_9001 with environmental-specific inputs (compliance obligations, environmental performance) | Low |
| IATF 16949:2016 | 9.3.1 (+9.3.1.1 IATF-specific inputs) | Automotive customer-specific requirements, cost of poor quality, warranty — real IATF-specific MR inputs beyond ISO 9001 | Medium |
| ISO 45001:2018 | 9.3 | Same HLS 9.3 structure again; OH&S incident/near-miss data as inputs | Low |
| AS9120B | 9.3 | Same aerospace-distributor delta pattern as AS9110C (counterfeit-parts control status, traceability) | Low |
| FAA Part 145 / EASA Part-145 | No formal "management review" clause, but Quality/Safety Management System review requirements exist | Repair stations already buying AS9110C-adjacent tools — bundle opportunity, not a new pack | Medium |
| ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 (food) | Food safety management review | Outside current portfolio focus (aerospace/medical/automotive) — defer unless food-GMP line of business grows | Low priority |

## Assessment
The HLS-based standards (14001, 45001) are nearly free to add — same clause 9.3 structure as
ISO 9001, differing only in a handful of domain-specific inputs. This is why they're already
first in the v1.2 roadmap. IATF and AS9120B require slightly more domain research (already
available from the IATF16949-auditor and AS9120B-auditor skills — reuse, don't rebuild) but are
still low-cost relative to the addressable market each unlocks.

## Not recommended
Do not build a food-safety pack until/unless a dedicated food-GMP product line justifies it —
this would be scope creep against the aerospace/medical/automotive core buyer MRA is built for.
