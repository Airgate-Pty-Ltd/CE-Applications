# Buyer, Pain, Willingness to Pay, and Pricing

## Target customer profile
**Primary:** Quality Manager / Management Representative, 20–500 employee certified
organization (ISO 9001, AS9100D, AS9110C, ISO 13485). Owns the MR calendar, personally
assembles the package, personally answers the CB auditor.
**Secondary:** QMS consultants running MR for multiple clients (multi-tenant tier).
**Tertiary:** Repair Station Accountable Managers (AS9110C/Part 145 overlap).

## Pain and willingness to pay
- Assembly cost: 2–5 days of a QM's time per cycle ≈ $1,500–$4,000 loaded cost, 1–4×/yr.
- Risk cost: an MR-related minor NC costs a corrective action cycle + surveillance
  scrutiny; a major can suspend certification — commercially existential for aerospace
  and medical suppliers.
- $79/mo ($948/yr) is <30% of ONE review cycle's labor cost. Consultants bill MR prep
  at $2k–5k per client; $199/mo across a client base is trivially justified.

## Subscription / service model
| Tier | Price | Includes |

| Professional | $79/mo | 1 standard, unlimited reviews, action-register carryover, print pack |

| Enterprise / Conform-Edge bundle | custom | bundled with Supplier Scorecard + Readiness Dashboard |

## Math to $250k+/yr
- 220 Professional × $948 = **$208,560**
- 25 Consultant × $2,388 = **$59,700**
- **Total ≈ $268,260/yr** (Starter tier and standard-revision upsells not counted)
- Feeder funnel: the 6 self-assessment tools already built (AS9100D, AS9110C, MDSAP,
  ISO 9001:2026, IATF, 8D) share the same buyer — cross-sell at output screens.

$79 anchor vs $99: test with first 20 customers. Consultants may prefer per-client
pricing ($15/client/mo) — revisit after 5 consultant signups.
