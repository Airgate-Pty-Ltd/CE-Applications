# Revenue Expansion — Management Review Autopilot

## Base case (from 03-Buyer-and-Pricing.md, verified this pass)
- 220 Professional ($79/mo) × $948/yr = $208,560
- 25 Multi-Standard/Consultant ($199/mo) × $2,388/yr = $59,700
- **Base total: $268,260/yr** at fewer than 250 customers, against a market of 1M+ certified orgs

## Expansion paths beyond the base case

1. **Consultant/OEM channel.** QMS consultants (Brian's own former market) running MR for
   multiple clients are a distribution channel, not just a customer segment — a consultant with
   10 clients on the $199 Multi-Standard tier and a 20% referral/reseller margin turns one signup
   into ten. Prioritize a consultant partner program once white-label export ships (v2.1).

2. **Cross-sell from the 6 self-assessment tools already built.** AS9100D, AS9110C, MDSAP,
   ISO 9001:2026 gap analyzer, IATF, and 8D workflow tool all end in an output screen — every one
   of those screens is a natural "now build your management review package" upsell. This is the
   single highest-leverage, lowest-cost revenue lever in the entire portfolio because the audience
   is identical and the products don't compete.

3. **ISO 9001:2026 transition event.** When the revised standard publishes (~Sept 2026 per
   roadmap), every certified org needs an MR package reflecting the new/changed clauses in the
   same review cycle they're already running. This is a natural forced-upgrade moment for the
   $79→$99 anchor test (03-Buyer-and-Pricing.md's open pricing question) — test the higher price
   specifically on transition-driven signups.

4. **Enterprise/multi-site bundle.** Organizations with multiple certified sites (common in
   aerospace and medical device manufacturing) need one MR package per site but want portfolio-
   level visibility — a multi-site rollup view is a natural Enterprise-tier feature once 3+
   Multi-Standard customers ask for it (build on demand, not speculatively).

5. **Annual pre-pay discount.** MR happens 1–4×/yr on a fixed calendar — this is exactly the kind
   of predictable-cadence product where annual pre-pay (e.g., 2 months free) improves cash flow
   and reduces churn risk around the "we didn't have a review this quarter, why are we paying"
   objection.

## Revised total addressable estimate
Base case ($268k) + conservative consultant-channel multiplier (10 consultants × 8 client
seats average, replacing the current flat 25-consultant assumption) pushes the realistic
Year-2 ceiling toward **$350–400k/yr** without any pricing change — the constraint is
distribution (channel + cross-sell wiring), not price or feature depth.
