# Product Concept Brief — Management Review Autopilot

## Product name
**Management Review Autopilot (MRA)** — a Conform-Edge product by Annex Quality.

## One-line
Turn raw QMS data into an audit-defensible management review package in under 30 minutes.

## Problem
Clause 9.3 (ISO 9001:2015, AS9100D, AS9110C) and 5.6 (ISO 13485:2016) require top
management to review the QMS at planned intervals considering a *prescribed* list of
inputs and producing a *prescribed* list of outputs. In practice:
- Inputs live in six different systems and four people's heads.
- The Quality Manager spends 2–5 days per cycle assembling the package.
- CB auditors sample MR records against the clause list; missing inputs and untracked
  output actions are among the most common findings.
- Most organizations reuse a stale slide deck that silently omits required inputs.

## Solution
A guided input board that mirrors the clause structure exactly:
1. **Select standard(s)** — ISO 9001:2015, AS9100D, AS9110C, ISO 13485:2016.
2. **Work the input board** — every required input appears as a card with status
   (Data entered / Missing / N/A with justification), fields for data, trend, and notes.
3. **Deterministic engine** scores completeness, flags audit exposure (which auditor
   finding each gap maps to), and refuses nothing — advisory only, human decides.
4. **Generate the package** — agenda, pre-populated minutes, executive summary,
   output-action register (all §9.3.3 outputs: improvement, QMS changes, resource needs
   — plus AS9100/13485 deltas), exported as Markdown/print.

## Differentiators
- **Evidence-calibrated, not template-ware:** each input card carries "what a CB auditor
  samples" guidance from real audit calibration — no competitor template does this.
- **Multi-standard deltas built in:** AS9100D on-time delivery & customer satisfaction
  emphases, AS9110C safety/human factors inputs, ISO 13485 regulatory reporting &
  advisory notices — one board, correct per standard.
- **Deterministic intelligence (M-AI aligned):** rules-only completeness engine;
  append-friendly action register; advisory outputs; human authority preserved.
- **[The Organization] placeholders** throughout so consultants can white-label outputs.

## Document architecture alignment
Generated minutes/records follow the standard architecture: Objective, Scope,
Responsibilities, Definitions, Method, References, Documented Information, Revision
History, Record of Review.
