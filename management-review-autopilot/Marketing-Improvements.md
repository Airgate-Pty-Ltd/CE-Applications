# Marketing Improvements — Management Review Autopilot

## Value proposition (sharpened)
**Before (executive summary framing):** "Converts raw QMS data into a complete, clause-compliant
management review package in under 30 minutes."

**Sharpened for landing page:** "Stop losing a week to management review. MRA turns the input
scramble into a guided 30-minute workflow — and shows you every gap a CB auditor would flag
*before* the review happens, not after the finding."

The sharpened version leads with the pain (a week lost) and the specific fear (the CB finding),
which is what actually motivates a QM to buy, more than the neutral "converts data" framing.

## Sales deck note
Given the buyer profile (QM/Management Rep, time-poor, audit-anxious), the sales narrative
should open with the CB-finding fear, not the software features — "missing MR inputs are a
top-5 CB finding" (already stated in 00-Executive-Summary.md) is the single strongest hook in
the existing materials and should lead every piece of marketing copy, not be buried mid-document.
