## What It Is
A self-contained HTML self-assessment tool for FAA Part 145 repair stations to identify SMS implementation gaps before regulatory requirements finalize. Based on ICAO Annex 19 / FAA AC 120-92B. Includes an automatic EASA bilateral advisory for MAG Change 10, Section 20 (SMSVP requirement).

## Target Buyer
Safety Manager / Quality Director at a US FAA Part 145 repair station — especially those with EASA bilateral approval, or serving major airline customers who require SMS evidence.

## Problem Solved
Most small-to-mid repair stations have never built an SMS and have no structured roadmap. This tool gives a guided gap assessment, component-by-component readiness verdict, and prioritized action list — without engaging a consultant first.

## Key Features
- 30 questions across 4 ICAO SMS components
- 4 critical-item overrides (mandatory minimum elements)
- READY / DEVELOPING / NOT-STARTED verdict with critical gap detection
- EASA bilateral advisory (MAG CHG 10 §20 SMSVP callout)
- Regulatory status notice (no final FAA rule yet — readiness framing)
- Prioritized gap table with clause references
- MR narrative + JSON export
- Offline, self-contained, no accounts
