⚠ Regulatory Status Notice: As of this tool's publication, the FAA has not issued a final mandatory SMS rule for Part 145 repair stations. This tool is designed for readiness preparation and gap identification — not compliance to a final rule. Organizations with EASA Part 145 bilateral approval are subject to SMS/SMSVP requirements per MAG Change 10, Section 20 (effective October 10, 2025). Airline customers may also require SMS evidence independently of regulatory mandates.
This self-assessment identifies SMS implementation gaps based on the ICAO Annex 19 / FAA AC 120-92B framework. Results are advisory. Your organization retains all safety determinations. Consult qualified safety and legal advisors before making compliance representations.
