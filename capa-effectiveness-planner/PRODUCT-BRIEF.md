# Product Brief — CAPA Effectiveness Verification Planner
**Brand:** Conform-Edge Quality Consulting LLC
**Route:** Software
**Status:** PROTOTYPE — launch blockers remain (see NEEDS-FROM-BRIAN.md)

## What It Is
A single-file HTML decision-support tool that transforms a completed corrective action into a structured effectiveness verification plan. Input: finding type, severity, root cause, CA type, and override flags (escape, safety, repeat). Output: verification pathway (CRITICAL/MAJOR/MINOR), recommended methods with evidence requirements, timeline, MR narrative, and JSON export.

## Target Buyer
Quality managers, CA owners, and compliance leads at AS9100D, ISO 9001, ISO 13485, IATF 16949, AS9110C, and FAA Part 145-registered organizations who need to document defensible effectiveness verification plans before CB, customer, or regulatory audits.

## Problem Solved
Clause 10.2.1(e)/8.5.2(f) requires effectiveness verification — most organizations close CAPAs on CA completion, not on verified effectiveness. CB auditors and customer auditors routinely cite this gap. This tool designs the verification plan from the CA type, eliminating the guess-work and ensuring evidence is planned before closure.
