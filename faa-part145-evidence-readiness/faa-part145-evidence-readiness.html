How to use this tool

For each evidence item, select Complete (documented, current, and retrievable), Partial (exists but has gaps or is outdated), Missing (not in place), or N/A (not applicable to your certificate/ratings). Items marked ★ Critical will set the domain to Not Ready if missing. Results show your readiness posture — they do not constitute an FAA determination or certificate readiness certification.
