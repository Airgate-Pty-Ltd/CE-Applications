# Product Brief — FAA Part 145 Evidence-Readiness Audit Tool
**Status:** Working prototype — pre-release-authorized (launch blockers outstanding)

## One-Line
Know exactly which evidence is missing before your FSDO surveillance or EASA Form 8 inspection.

## Target Buyer
Quality Manager / Director of Maintenance / Accountable Manager at an FAA Part 145 Certificated Repair Station — domestic or foreign-certificated, with or without EASA bilateral.

## What It Does
Walks you through 10 Part 145 evidence domains (~40 items). You mark each piece of evidence COMPLETE, PARTIAL, MISSING, or N/A. The tool immediately shows you which domains are READY, which are CONDITIONAL, and which are NOT READY — with a prioritized remediation list so you fix the highest-risk gaps first. EASA bilateral stations get a MAG Change 10 §20 advisory.
