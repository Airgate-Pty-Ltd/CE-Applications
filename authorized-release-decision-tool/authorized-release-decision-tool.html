Customer Requirement & Destination Authority
BASA TIP / MAG
What regulatory authority does the customer / end operator require release for?Critical
Does your organization hold a bilateral approval that enables release under the destination authority?
FAA Part 145 repair stations may hold EASA bilateral approval (US-EU BASA / MAG). PAHs may have bilateral production recognition. Without bilateral authorization, you cannot sign an EASA Form 1 as the approving organization.
3
Article Eligibility & Limitations
Order 8130.21 / AMC 145.A.50
Is the article an eligible aircraft article (airframe, engine, propeller, appliance, part, or component of a civil aircraft)?
FAA 8130-3 and EASA Form 1 are for aircraft articles only. Ground support equipment, tooling, and test equipment do not receive airworthiness releases — they use CoC or calibration certificates.
Are there any known airworthiness limitations, mandatory replacement life limits, or open ADs that affect this article?Critical
Life-limited parts (LLPs), time-controlled components, and articles subject to unresolved ADs cannot be released as airworthy without resolving or disclosing the limitation on the tag.
Has the maintenance / repair / overhaul work been completed and inspected in accordance with approved data?
For repair stations: "approved data" means manufacturer CMM/AMM, FAA-approved repair specification, ATA standards, or other data accepted under 14 CFR Part 43. Work performed to unapproved data cannot receive an 8130-3.
4
Certifying Authority
14 CFR §145.211 / EASA Part-145.A.50
Is the individual approving the release authorized by your organization's RSM/QCM to sign the release document for this article type and rating?Critical
FAA 8130-3: must be an authorized representative per §145.211 for the specific work performed within the repair station's ratings. EASA Form 1: must be EASA certifying staff with appropriate Part-66 authorization (where required) or organization authorization scheme entry.
Will a return-to-service (RTS) statement be required on the document in addition to the approval block?
FAA 8130-3 Block 12 for maintenance work includes the RTS statement. EASA Form 1 Box 14 is the certification statement. New parts from PAHs do not include an RTS statement — they certify conformity.
