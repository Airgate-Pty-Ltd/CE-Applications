Calibration & OOT Impact Manager

Conform-Edge · Out-of-Tolerance Reverse-Traceability Engine
Self-contained. No data leaves this page.

When a gauge or instrument comes back out of tolerance (OOT) at calibration, AS9100 7.1.5.2 requires you to assess and act on the validity of previous measuring results. This tool runs a deterministic reverse-traceability decision: it weighs the OOT magnitude against your measurement guard-band, ranks affected product between last-known-good and discovery, and outputs a recall/containment recommendation with the required records.
