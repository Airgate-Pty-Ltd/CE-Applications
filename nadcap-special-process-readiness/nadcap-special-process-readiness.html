About This Tool

NADCAP (National Aerospace and Defense Contractors Accreditation Program) accreditation is a mandatory requirement from most major aerospace OEMs (Boeing, Airbus, Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, and others). This self-assessment helps manufacturers identify gaps before a NADCAP audit across the most common special process categories: Heat Treatment, Non-Destructive Testing (NDT/NDE), Chemical Processing, Welding, and Composites.

Each process section maps to the applicable NADCAP audit criteria (AC7xxx series). Critical items marked CRITICAL represent findings likely to trigger a NADCAP audit NCR or process audit delay. A single critical NO triggers a NOT READY verdict regardless of overall score.

Process Selection

Select a process to load its specific NADCAP audit checklist. Complete all questions, then generate your readiness verdict.

Heat Treatment Readiness — AC7102

Applies to solution heat treatment, aging, annealing, normalizing, quenching, and stress relieving of metals. Covers aerospace alloy specifications (AMS, MIL), furnace qualification, and process control. NADCAP audit criteria: AC7102/1–AC7102/9 series.
Critical — single NO = NOT READY
Standard
Section 1 — Specifications & Process Procedures
HT-1Are all applicable material and process specifications (AMS, MIL-H, customer-specific) listed in a controlled document and currently revision-controlled?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7102 §2.1 / AS9100D Cl. 8.5.1 / Customer engineering drawing requirement
HT-2Is there a written Heat Treatment Process Procedure (HTPP) or shop traveler for each process type performed, cross-referencing the governing specification?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7102 §3.1 / AMS2750 §5 / AS9100D Cl. 8.5.1
HT-3Are process procedures reviewed and approved by qualified personnel before use, and re-reviewed when process parameters or specifications change?
Ref: AC7102 §3.2 / AS9100D Cl. 7.5
Section 2 — Furnace Qualification (AMS2750)
HT-4Are all furnaces used for aerospace heat treatment classified (Type, Class) per AMS2750 and are current Temperature Uniformity Survey (TUS) records on file and within expiry?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7102 §4.1 / AMS2750F §3.4 TUS requirements
HT-5Are System Accuracy Tests (SATs) performed at the required frequency per AMS2750 and are SAT records current with no expired sensors in use?CRITICAL
Ref: AMS2750F §3.3 SAT requirements / AC7102 §4.2
HT-6Are thermocouples calibrated, lot-tested, and replaced per AMS2750 requirements, with calibration records traceable to NIST/NPL?
Ref: AMS2750F §3.2 / AC7102 §4.3
HT-7Are furnace instrumentation and controllers (recorders, controllers) calibrated per AMS2750 and are calibration records maintained?
Ref: AMS2750F §3.1 / AS9100D Cl. 7.1.5
Section 3 — Personnel Qualification
HT-8Are heat treatment operators trained and authorized for each process type, with training records on file showing task-specific authorization?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7102 §5.1 / AS9100D Cl. 7.2 / Customer flow-down requirements
HT-9Is there a designated responsible individual (process supervisor or metallurgist) accountable for heat treatment process compliance?
Ref: AC7102 §5.2 / AS9100D Cl. 5.3
Section 4 — Load Documentation & Records
HT-10Is a load record (furnace log or shop traveler) completed for every heat treatment run, capturing: part numbers, alloy/specification, set-point vs. actual temperatures, time-at-temperature, operator ID, and furnace ID?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7102 §6.1 / AMS2750 data recording requirements / AS9100D Cl. 8.5.2
HT-11Are heat treatment records retained per the applicable record retention requirement (typically 10+ years for aerospace) and are records readily retrievable by part number and lot?
Ref: AC7102 §6.2 / AS9100D Cl. 7.5.3 / Customer PO requirements
Section 5 — Nonconforming Material & Corrective Action
HT-12Is there a documented process for identifying and segregating nonconforming heat-treated parts, including notification to customer when specification deviations occur?
Ref: AC7102 §7 / AS9100D Cl. 8.7 / Customer notification requirements
HT-13Are process deviations and equipment anomalies investigated with root cause and corrective action, and are findings tracked to closure?
Ref: AC7102 §7.2 / AS9100D Cl. 10.2

Non-Destructive Testing / NDE Readiness — AC7114

Applies to penetrant testing (PT), magnetic particle testing (MT), ultrasonic testing (UT), radiographic testing (RT), and eddy current testing (ET). Personnel qualification per NAS 410 / EN 4179. NADCAP criteria: AC7114/1–AC7114/9 series.
Critical — single NO = NOT READY
Standard
Section 1 — Personnel Qualification (NAS 410 / EN 4179)
NDT-1Are all NDT/NDE personnel qualified and certified to NAS 410 or EN 4179 (or customer-specified alternative) at the required level (Level I/II/III) for each method and technique performed?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7114 §3.1 / NAS 410 Rev 5 / Customer NDT qualification requirements
NDT-2Is there a written and approved NDT Written Practice covering: methods used, qualification requirements, training requirements, experience requirements, examination requirements, and certification/recertification cycle?CRITICAL
Ref: NAS 410 §6.1 Written Practice / AC7114 §3.2
NDT-3Are current certification records maintained for each NDT technician showing: method, technique, level, initial cert date, expiry date, and employer/certifying entity?
Ref: NAS 410 §6.4 / AC7114 §3.3
Section 2 — Procedures & Specifications
NDT-4Is each NDT method performed per a written, approved procedure (per ASTM, AMS, or customer process spec) that has been reviewed by a Level III and is currently revision-controlled?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7114 §4.1 / ASTM E1444 / AMS2647 / Customer flow-down
NDT-5Are NDT procedures qualified/demonstrated before first use on production parts, with qualification records retained?
Ref: AC7114 §4.2 / AS9100D Cl. 8.5.1
Section 3 — Equipment & Reference Standards
NDT-6Are NDT equipment and reference standards (penetrant test panels, mag particle reference bars, UT reference blocks, RT penetrameters) calibrated/verified per procedure requirements and within calibration intervals?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7114 §5.1 / AS9100D Cl. 7.1.5 / ASTM method-specific calibration requirements
NDT-7Is NDT equipment (UV lights, ammeters, ultrasonic instruments) qualified for each applicable technique before use, with qualification records maintained?
Ref: AC7114 §5.2 / ASTM E709 / ASTM E1444
Section 4 — Process Control & Records
NDT-8Is an NDT inspection record (report/traveler) generated for each part/lot inspected, capturing: date, part ID, method, technique, procedure revision, acceptance criteria, result (accept/reject), and Level II/III certifier signature?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7114 §6.1 / AS9100D Cl. 8.6 / Customer CoC and record requirements
NDT-9Are penetrant and magnetic particle processing chemicals controlled (concentration/bath monitoring, concentration logs, contamination checks) per procedure requirements?
Ref: AC7114 §4.3 (PT/MT chemistry control) / AMS2647 / ASTM E1444
NDT-10Is NDT work environment (lighting, temperature, cleanliness) verified before each inspection session with verification records retained?
Ref: AC7114 §5.3 / ASTM E1444 lighting requirements

Chemical Processing Readiness — AC7108

Applies to anodizing, plating, chemical conversion coating, etching, cleaning, and passivation of aerospace parts. NADCAP criteria: AC7108 series. Environmental and worker safety controls are audited alongside process controls.
Critical — single NO = NOT READY
Standard
Section 1 — Specification & Process Control
CH-1Is each chemical process performed to a current, controlled process specification (AMS, MIL, customer spec) and are all applicable flow-down requirements identified and traceable to the part/process record?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7108 §2.1 / AMS2488 / MIL-A-8625 / Customer PO engineering requirements
CH-2Are bath or solution concentrations (chemical tank chemistry) monitored at the required frequency, with bath analysis records maintained and out-of-range actions documented?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7108 §3.1 / Process specification bath control requirements
CH-3Are process parameters (temperature, voltage/current, time, pH) monitored and recorded for each production run, with parameter ranges defined and deviations managed?
Ref: AC7108 §3.2 / AMS process specification parameter ranges
Section 2 — Personnel & Environmental Controls
CH-4Are chemical processing personnel trained for each process they perform, with task-specific training authorization on record?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7108 §4.1 / AS9100D Cl. 7.2
CH-5Are environmental and safety requirements (waste treatment, discharge limits, PPE, HazMat management) documented, controlled, and compliant with applicable permits?
Ref: AC7108 §4.2 / EPA / Customer environmental requirements
CH-6Are masking materials and methods controlled and approved, with masking records showing which areas were masked for each part?
Ref: AC7108 §3.3 / Customer masking approval requirements
Section 3 — Testing & Inspection
CH-7Is coating thickness, adhesion, or other process verification testing performed as required by specification, with test results recorded per part/lot and instruments calibrated?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7108 §5.1 / AMS specification acceptance test requirements / AS9100D Cl. 7.1.5
CH-8Are process records (traveler/log) completed for each batch or part showing: part numbers, specification, tank/bath ID, parameters, results, and operator/inspector ID?
Ref: AC7108 §5.2 / AS9100D Cl. 8.5.2
CH-9Is test panel / witness coupon testing performed as required by specification, with results meeting acceptance criteria and records retained?
Ref: AC7108 §5.3 / MIL-A-8625 test panel requirements

Welding Readiness — AC7110

Applies to fusion welding, resistance welding, and brazing on aerospace structural components. Welder qualification per AWS D17.1 / AMS2680 / customer specification. NADCAP criteria: AC7110 series.
Critical — single NO = NOT READY
Standard
Section 1 — Welder / Operator Qualification
W-1Are all welders and welding operators qualified and currently certified to the applicable welding standard (AWS D17.1, AMS2680, customer spec) for each process, position, and base metal they weld?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7110 §3.1 / AWS D17.1 / Customer welding qualification requirements
W-2Are current welder qualification records maintained showing: process, position, base metal group, filler metal, effective date, expiry, and continuity evidence (recent production welds)?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7110 §3.2 / AWS D17.1 §5 qualification record requirements
Section 2 — Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS)
W-3Is each weld joint type performed per a written, approved Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) that has been qualified with a Procedure Qualification Record (PQR) and is currently revision-controlled?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7110 §4.1 / AWS D17.1 §4 WPS/PQR requirements
W-4Are welding procedure specification changes controlled so that essential variable changes trigger re-qualification?
Ref: AC7110 §4.2 / AWS D17.1 essential variable requirements
Section 3 — Filler Metals & Consumables
W-5Are filler metals and welding consumables procured to AMS or AWS specification, received with certified test reports (C of C + chemical/mechanical test data), and stored per specification requirements (temperature, humidity)?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7110 §5.1 / AMS filler metal specifications / AWS consumable requirements
W-6Is filler metal traceability maintained from receipt through to the weld joint (heat/lot number recorded on weld traveler)?
Ref: AC7110 §5.2 / AS9100D Cl. 8.5.2 traceability
Section 4 — Inspection & NDT
W-7Is weld inspection performed per documented criteria (visual inspection to AWS D17.1 Class A/B/C or customer acceptance criteria) with qualified inspectors and inspection records per joint?
Ref: AC7110 §6.1 / AWS D17.1 §8 acceptance criteria
W-8Are weld repair procedures documented and controlled, with repair requirements satisfied (qualified welder, re-inspection, customer notification if required)?
Ref: AC7110 §6.2 / AS9100D Cl. 8.7 nonconforming output

Composites Readiness — AC7118

Applies to lay-up and cure of thermoset composite structures (carbon fibre/epoxy, fibreglass) for aerospace applications. Material control, autoclave/oven qualification, and cure documentation are the primary audit focus. NADCAP criteria: AC7118 series.
Critical — single NO = NOT READY
Standard
Section 1 — Prepreg Material Control
CO-1Are prepreg materials procured to a qualified material specification (AMS, customer QPL), stored at the required temperature, and tracked for cumulative out-time and freezer-time against expiry limits?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7118 §3.1 / Customer material control requirements / AMS specification storage requirements
CO-2Is material traceability maintained from prepreg roll receipt through layup and cure, with lot/roll numbers recorded on the traveler so finished-part traceability to raw material is possible?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7118 §3.2 / AS9100D Cl. 8.5.2 traceability
Section 2 — Autoclave / Oven Qualification
CO-3Are autoclaves and ovens used for composite cure qualified per AMS2750 (temperature uniformity) requirements applicable to composites cure equipment, with current TUS/SAT records on file?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7118 §4.1 / AMS2750 cure equipment applicability / Customer equipment qualification requirements
CO-4Are autoclave pressure gauges, thermocouples, and control/recording instruments calibrated per AMS2750 requirements with calibration records maintained?
Ref: AC7118 §4.2 / AMS2750 instrumentation requirements
Section 3 — Layup & Cure Process Controls
CO-5Is each composite part produced per a controlled layup and cure procedure that specifies: ply sequence, fibre orientation, cure cycle (time, temperature, pressure ramp rates), vacuum requirements, and acceptance criteria?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7118 §5.1 / Drawing/manufacturing planning requirements / AS9100D Cl. 8.5.1
CO-6Are layup operators trained and authorized for composite layup tasks with task-specific training records on file?
Ref: AC7118 §5.2 / AS9100D Cl. 7.2
CO-7Is a cure cycle traveler completed for every autoclave/oven run capturing: part numbers, lay-up operator, cure cycle set-points vs. actuals (temp, pressure, vacuum, time), thermocouple data, and cure completion sign-off?CRITICAL
Ref: AC7118 §5.3 / Customer cure record requirements
CO-8Are test panels / witness coupons cured with each production lot per specification requirements, with mechanical/NDT test results recorded and meeting acceptance criteria?
Ref: AC7118 §5.4 / Customer test coupon specification requirements
