# Product Brief — AS9102B FAI Self-Scoring Toolkit

**Brand:** Conform-Edge Quality Consulting
**Product type:** Static HTML self-scoring tool
**Date:** 2026-08-29

---

## What It Is

A two-mode self-scoring HTML tool for aerospace manufacturers to assess First Article Inspection (FAI) documentation completeness before customer submission or CB audit review.

**Mode 1 — FAIR Completeness Check:** Scores 14 documentation elements against AS9102 Rev B §5 requirements. Critical items override the aggregate score. Produces FAIR-DOCUMENTATION-COMPLETE / GAPS / INCOMPLETE verdict with prioritized gap table.

**Mode 2 — Delta/Partial FAI Trigger Analysis:** 8-question change-event analysis to determine whether a new full FAI, partial FAI, or no new FAI is required per AS9102 Rev B §4.2.

---

## Buyer

Quality Director or Quality Engineer at an AS9100D-registered aerospace supplier (Tier 1/2). Runs the tool before submitting a FAIR to catch documentation gaps before customer or CB review.
