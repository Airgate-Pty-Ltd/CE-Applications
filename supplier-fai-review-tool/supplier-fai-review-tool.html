Supplier FAIR Review Tool

AS9102B — OEM / Receiving Organisation Incoming FAIR Package Review

Review Setup

Delta FAI: Review covers only characteristics and documentation affected by the change. Confirm the change scope is documented in Form 1 and unchanged characteristics are substantiated from the previous full FAIR.
Partial FAI: Supplier must identify and substantiate all AS9102B requirements satisfied by the partial. Review for completeness against the agreed partial scope.
Section 1 — Form 1: Design Documentation Identification
1
Part number and drawing revision level on Form 1 match the purchase order and current design release. ⚑ Critical
AS9102B §4.1; Form 1 Block 2, 3, 4
2
FAI Part Number Accountability section (Form 1) includes all sub-components, assemblies, and fasteners as applicable.
AS9102B §4.2; Form 1 Block 5-11
3
Accountable Manager / Authorized Release Signatory signature and date are present on Form 1.
AS9102B §4.1.2; Form 1 Block 20
4
FAI date and FAI Part Number on Form 1 are consistent with all other forms submitted.
AS9102B §4.1; Form 1 Block 1, 2
5
(Delta FAI) Change description is documented in Form 1; scope of affected characteristics is clearly identified and bounded.
AS9102B §3.1 Delta FAI; Form 1 remarks block
Section 2 — Form 2: Drawing & Specification Accountability
6
Ballooned drawing is present, at the current revision, with all dimensions/characteristics numbered and traceable to Form 4. ⚑ Critical
AS9102B §4.2; Form 2
7
Design documentation list on Form 2 includes all applicable drawings, specifications, and standards referenced in the design data.
AS9102B §4.2.1
8
All customer-identified engineering change orders (ECOs / EOs) or design change notices are reflected at their correct revision in Form 2.
AS9102B §4.2.1; design data currency
Section 3 — Form 3: Materials, Chemical, Physical & Functional Tests
9
Material certifications (CoCs / mill certs) are present, traceable to the raw material lot used for the first article, and reference the applicable material specification at correct revision. ⚑ Critical
AS9102B §4.3.1; Form 3 Block 2
10
Special processes are performed by qualified sources. Qualified Manufacturer List (QPL/PAS) approval is current at time of FAI and documented. ⚑ Critical
AS9102B §4.3.2; Form 3 Block 3
11
Chemical analysis and/or physical test reports are present for all design-required tests (tensile, hardness, conductivity, etc.) with pass/fail verdicts.
AS9102B §4.3.3; Form 3 Block 4
12
Functional / performance test results are documented per design requirements, with acceptance criteria stated and pass/fail verdicts recorded.
AS9102B §4.3.4; Form 3 Block 5
Section 4 — Form 4: Characteristic Accountability & Dimensional Results
13
All balloon numbers from the ballooned drawing are accounted for in Form 4 — 100% accountability, with no characteristics left unmeasured without documented disposition. ⚑ Critical
AS9102B §4.4.1; Form 4 100% accountability rule
14
Actual measurement values (not just pass/fail) are recorded for each characteristic with nominal, tolerance, and actual value columns populated.
AS9102B §4.4.2; Form 4 column requirements
15
Key Characteristics (KCs) are identified on Form 4 and have measurement data with Cpk index (or justified alternative for low-volume KCs). All KC results are within spec. ⚑ Critical
AS9102B §4.4.3; Key Characteristic accountability
16
Gauge / measurement tool identification is referenced (gauge number or CMM report reference) for each measurement in Form 4, and calibration status is current.
AS9102B §4.4.4; measurement traceability
17
All out-of-tolerance or non-conforming characteristics are identified with an N/C flag and dispositioned (concession, rework, scrap) before FAI submission.
AS9102B §4.4.5; NC disposition
Section 5 — Form 5: FAI Disposition & Supplier Approval
18
Form 5 FAI Disposition block is completed: FAIR Accepted / FAIR Accepted with conditions / FAIR Rejected — with authorized signature and date. ⚑ Critical
AS9102B §4.5; Form 5 FAI disposition
19
Conformance Statement is signed by an authorized representative of the supplier confirming the first article is representative of production tooling, equipment, and processes.
AS9102B §4.5.1; Form 5 conformance statement
20
All conditions or open action items noted on Form 5 (if FAIR Accepted with Conditions) have a documented closure path with dates and responsible party.
AS9102B §4.5.2; conditional acceptance tracking
Section 6 — Production Representativeness & Supplier Status
21
Supplier is on the Approved Supplier List (ASL) and approval status is current. Quality Management certification (AS9100D/ISO 9001 etc.) is valid at time of FAI.
AS9102B §3.2; supplier approval status
22
First article part is confirmed as production-representative: manufactured using production tooling, production equipment, and production processes — no prototype processes used.
AS9102B §3.3; production representativeness
23
Part serial number / lot number of the first article is recorded and traceable through all forms to the actual hardware.
AS9102B §4.1; traceability
24
Record retention statement or plan is identified for the FAIR package (typically life of part / product, per customer or regulatory requirement).
AS9102B §3.4; record retention
25
No previously-open corrective actions (SCARs) from this supplier related to this part family are outstanding that would affect FAIR validity.
AS9102B §3.2; supplier quality history
