## Section X.1 — Integrated Management Framework

### X.1.1 Policy Statement

[AMO NAME] is committed to maintaining the highest standards of safety and quality in all maintenance activities conducted under CAAC CCAR-145 authorization [PLACEHOLDER: authorization number].

The Accountable Manager has established this Integrated Management Policy affirming that:

1. **Safety is the first priority.** No production or commercial pressure will compromise the safety of maintenance activities or the airworthiness of released aircraft/components.

2. **Quality and Safety are unified.** Our Quality Management System and Safety Management System are managed as a single integrated framework. Quality standards are the baseline; safety requirements build on and extend them.

3. **All personnel are accountable.** Every person at [AMO NAME] — regardless of role — contributes to safety and quality outcomes. Accountability is matched by authority to stop unsafe work.

4. **Reporting is encouraged.** We operate a Just Culture. Personnel who identify and report hazards, errors, or safety concerns in good faith are protected from punitive action.

5. **We learn and improve.** Safety and quality data are analyzed for trends. Lessons learned are communicated and acted upon.

This policy is reviewed annually and whenever there is a significant change to our organization, operations, or the regulatory environment.

## Section X.3 — Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities

### X.3.1 Accountable Manager
The Accountable Manager holds ultimate responsibility for both the Quality Management System and the Safety Management System. Under CCAR-145 R4, the Accountable Manager specifically:

- Signs and owns the Integrated Management Policy
- Chairs the Safety Review Board (if applicable)
- Reviews and approves Safety Performance Monitoring Reports
- Ensures adequate resources are allocated to both quality and safety functions
- Has authority to halt operations that pose unacceptable safety risk

### X.3.2 Quality Manager / Director of Quality
- Owns the day-to-day operation of the integrated QMS+SMS
- Manages the internal audit programme (including safety criteria)
- Tracks quality and safety performance indicators
- Reports to the Accountable Manager on integrated system performance
- Under R4: Serves as or appoints the Safety Manager function (see X.3.3)

## Section X.6 — Just Culture and Voluntary Reporting

[AMO NAME] operates a Just Culture. The following protections apply to voluntary safety reporting:

- Any employee may report a hazard, near-miss, incident, or safety concern without fear of punishment, provided the report is made in good faith.
- Reports may be submitted anonymously via [PLACEHOLDER: reporting channel — paper form / digital system / email].
- All reports are reviewed within [PLACEHOLDER: 5 / 10] business days.
- The reporter (if identified and consenting) receives feedback on action taken within [PLACEHOLDER: 30] days.
- Deliberate violations, gross negligence, and criminal acts are excluded from Just Culture protection.
