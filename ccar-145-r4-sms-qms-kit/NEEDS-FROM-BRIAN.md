## Critical (Required Before Sale)

1. **CCAR-145 R4 Official Publication Date and Transition Deadline**  
   The single most important data point buyers will ask: when does R4 take effect, and how long do they have to comply? This needs to come from the official CAAC document or your class materials from Beijing. Without this, the kit is technically incomplete.  
   → Insert into: `01-ccar-145-r4-change-summary.md` (Section 5) and `00-overview.md`

2. **R4 Applicability Scope**  
   Does R4 apply to all CCAR-145 AMO categories (A1, A2, A3, B1, B2, C, D)? Or only categories above a certain size or type? Your class materials should have this.  
   → Insert into: `00-overview.md` and `04-r4-readiness-checklist.md`

3. **Safety Review Board Threshold**  
   R4 appears to require a Safety Review Board for AMOs above a threshold size. What is that threshold (number of authorized personnel, number of authorizations, revenue, etc.)?  
   → Insert into: `02-sms-qms-gap-matrix.md` (item A9), `03-integrated-manual-template.md` (Section X.3.1)

4. **Risk Matrix Dimensions**  
   Confirm whether CAAC R4 mandates a specific severity × likelihood matrix (5×5 assumed in this build) or leaves the methodology to the AMO.  
   → Insert into: `02-sms-qms-gap-matrix.md` and `03-integrated-manual-template.md` (Section X.4.2)

5. **CAAC Submission Requirement for SMS Documentation**  
   Does R4 require AMOs to submit/notify CAAC when they've integrated SMS into their QMS, or is it self-implemented and verified at surveillance? This affects Checklist item 6.04.  
   → Insert into: `04-r4-readiness-checklist.md` (item 6.04)
