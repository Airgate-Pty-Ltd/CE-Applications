## What CCAR-145 R4 Changes

CAAC CCAR-145 Revision 4 formally integrates **Safety Management System (SMS)** requirements into the existing **Quality Management System (QMS)** framework for Approved Maintenance Organizations (AMOs) operating under Chinese civil aviation authority.

This is a material change from prior revisions, which treated QMS and SMS as parallel but separate systems. R4 establishes:

- A **unified management framework** where SMS becomes embedded within the QMS structure
- Explicit **Safety Performance Indicators (SPIs)** required alongside quality metrics
- **Safety assurance processes** that mirror (but extend) quality audit functions
- **Safety promotion** obligations that align with (but go beyond) training requirements
- New documentation standards for **integrated management manuals**

For Western-trained quality professionals and MROs with CAAC authorization, R4 creates an urgent compliance need with almost no English-language guidance available.

## How to Use This Kit

**Step 1 — Read the Change Summary (01)**  
Understand what R4 requires before assessing your current state.

**Step 2 — Run the Gap Matrix (02)**  
Rate each requirement: Fully Met / Partially Met / Not Met. Score generates a RAG heat map of compliance posture.

**Step 3 — Review the Manual Template (03)**  
Use as the basis for updating your AMO Manual's management system section.

**Step 4 — Run the Readiness Checklist (04)**  
48-item pass/fail self-assessment. Items that fail generate a corrective action list.

**Step 5 — Build your implementation plan**  
Prioritize gaps by risk (safety-critical first, documentation second). Target the CAAC compliance date.
