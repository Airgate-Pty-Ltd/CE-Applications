## Opportunity Validation
- **Market:** All ISO 9001/AS9100/AS9110/IATF/ISO 13485-registered organizations. §7.2 (Competence) is one of the top 5 most-cited NC clauses across all CB audit programs.

- **Differentiator:** Deterministic competence gap detection per role + training effectiveness evaluation (Kirkpatrick Level 1-2 proxy) + evidence record generation for §7.2 management review. Bundles naturally with AuditProgramIQ.
- **Revenue:** $199/yr standalone; $399/yr bundle with AuditProgramIQ; $699/yr consultant license
- **Path:** ~$260k+/yr at scale
