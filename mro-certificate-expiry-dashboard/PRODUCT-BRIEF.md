# Product Brief — MRO Certificate Expiry Intelligence Dashboard
**Brand:** Conform-Edge Quality Consulting
**Date:** 2026-08-27

---

## What It Is

A self-contained HTML dashboard that tracks repair station certificate and approval renewal deadlines across six regulatory authorities (FAA, EASA, CAAC, UK CAA, CASA, TCCA). It applies a 5-tier urgency engine and CAAC Dec-31 hard-deadline logic to give Quality Directors an at-a-glance station posture and per-certificate action guidance — then generates a management review narrative and JSON export.

## Who It's For

Quality Directors and Operations Managers at multi-authority Part 145 repair stations who currently track cert renewals in spreadsheets or not at all. The target user juggles 4–10 simultaneous approvals across authorities with different renewal cycles, lead times, and inspection requirements.

## The Problem It Solves

Missing a single certificate renewal can ground all operations under that authority. Most Part 145 stations have no systematic multi-authority expiry tracker. Building one in spreadsheets is fragile and non-auditable.
