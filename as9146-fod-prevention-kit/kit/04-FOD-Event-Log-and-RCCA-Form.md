## RCCA Trigger Rules (deterministic)
- FO found **inside product/assembly** → mandatory RCCA (major).
- Repeated FO from **same source** (≥2 in rolling 90 days) → systemic RCCA.
- Tool/hardware **unaccounted for** → immediate containment + RCCA.
- Single loose FO in Normal zone, no product exposure → log + trend only.

## RCCA (attach site CAPA form)
Problem statement, containment, root cause (validated method), corrective action, effectiveness verification, systemic read-across to similar areas.
