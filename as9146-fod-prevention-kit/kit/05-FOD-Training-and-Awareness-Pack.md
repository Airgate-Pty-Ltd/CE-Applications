# FOD Training & Awareness Pack — QP-FOD-001 Attachment D

## Learning Objectives
1. Define FO and FOD and why they threaten airworthiness and cost.
2. Apply zone-appropriate controls (tool accountability, clean-as-you-go, consumable control).
3. Execute the 3-level FOD inspection.
4. Report and contain an FO find correctly.

## Core Content (talking points)
- Real-world FOD consequences: scrapped hardware, escape to customer, safety-of-flight.
- The FO lifecycle: introduced → migrates → causes damage → escapes. Break the chain early.
- "If you drop it, find it. If you can't find it, stop." — lost-item discipline.
- Evidence culture: a clean area with no records is not demonstrable in an audit.
