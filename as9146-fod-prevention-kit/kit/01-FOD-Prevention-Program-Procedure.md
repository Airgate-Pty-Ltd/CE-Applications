# Foreign Object Damage (FOD) Prevention Program
**Aligned to AS9146 (intent) · [The Organization] · Doc: QP-FOD-001 · Rev [X]**

---

## 1. Purpose
Establish a controlled program to prevent, detect, and remove foreign objects (FO) and mitigate Foreign Object Damage (FOD) across manufacturing, assembly, inspection, packaging, and maintenance operations.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all areas, personnel, contractors, and visitors where product, tooling, or hardware is exposed. References AS9146 program elements by intent; the invoked customer/contract requirement governs where stricter.

## 3. Definitions
- **Foreign Object (FO):** any item alien to the product/process that could cause damage.
- **FOD:** damage (or the cost of preventing it) attributed to a foreign object.
- **FOD-Critical Area:** zone where FO presence poses high risk to product or safety.

## 4. Roles
- **FOD Program Manager:** owns the program, metrics, and management review input.
- **Area Supervisors:** enforce zoning, tool control, and clean-as-you-go in their area.
- **All Personnel:** report and remove FO; comply with tool accountability.

## 5. Program Elements
### 5.1 FOD Risk Zoning
Classify each area as Normal, FOD-Aware, or FOD-Critical [PLACEHOLDER: site zoning map]. Controls escalate by zone (see Zoning Matrix, doc 02).

### 5.2 Tool & Hardware Accountability
Shadow boards / tool tethering / consumable logs in Critical zones; reconcile tools before closing any assembly. Lost-item procedure triggers a controlled stop-and-search.

### 5.3 Clean-As-You-Go
Housekeeping standard with defined frequency per zone; end-of-shift FOD walk.

### 5.4 Material & Consumable Control
Control fasteners, lock-wire offcuts, drill swarf, tape, gloves, and packaging debris at point of use.

### 5.5 Inspection & Detection
Defined FOD checks at stage transitions and before closure/shipment (see Checklist, doc 03).

### 5.6 Reporting, Containment & RCCA
Any FO found or FOD event is logged, contained, and root-caused using the site CAPA method; recurring FO sources drive systemic action.

### 5.7 Training & Awareness
Initial + recurrent FOD training for all personnel; visitor briefing for Critical zones (see Training, doc 05).

## 6. Metrics
FO finds by zone/source, FOD events, open RCCA aging, audit pass rate — reported to management review.
