## 1. Relationship rule (governing)

1. **Reference-only.** Reviewed builds are read as design and lessons references. They are not sources of code.
2. **No code copy.** No file, function, schema file, JSON, checklist row or test is copied into the Audit Toolkit. Concepts may inform an independently written design.
3. **No undeclared dependency.** The Toolkit imports, links, vendors or calls nothing from these builds. Any future dependency needs a written Decision Log entry naming the interface and the approver.
4. **Independent product.** The Toolkit has its own repo, build, tests, data model, release path and brand (Conform-Edge only). It works with none of the other products present.
5. **Clean brand and content.** No Annex Quality or Burrana branding, data, checklists or fixtures. No standard text unless public domain or licensed for redistribution.

1. **Strongest overlap is Pocket Auditor** (same platform, intent-based, offline, M-AI). The Toolkit must be differentiated by a written scope statement before build, or it risks being a second Pocket Auditor.

4. **Content risk is concentrated in Pocket Auditor** (`licensed-private\`, Burrana-derived checklists). The Toolkit must not mount, index or test against that folder.
5. **Duplicates to resolve (not by deletion):** two AuditProgramIQ builds (08-20, 09-07) with conflicting pricing; three diverged copies of the AS9100 audit tool repo. Owner decision, per vault CLAUDE.md Deletion Rule.
