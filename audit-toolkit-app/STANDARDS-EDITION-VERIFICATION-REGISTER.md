## 3. Licensing (high level, not legal advice)

- **ISO/IEC standards** (9001, 14001, 27001, 13485, 45001, 22000, 19011, 17021-1): copyrighted; typically single-user, non-transferable, watermarked licences; sharing, copying, intranet/network use needs a separate multi-user/network licence via ISO or national members (ISO help centre, SEARCH: https://help.iso.org/en/articles/376041-can-i-post-the-standard-i-have-purchased-on-the-intranet ). National adoptions (BSI, DIN, EVS, SIS) carry their own terms (e.g. DIN network licences: https://dinmedia.de/en/network-licences ).
- **SAE standards** (AS9100/9110/9120/9101/9104): SAE online store sells single-user; multi-user via SAE sales (SEARCH). SAE licence text not retrieved: UNVERIFIED.
- **IATF 16949**: IATF copyright; AIAG/resellers. SIs and FAQs free.
- **Implication for the product**: no verbatim standard text redistributed in a shipped app; clause-number references and original paraphrase only; confirm with counsel and the publishers before any content or data packaging that quotes or closely mirrors text.
- **Free sources of the standards**: none identified for any item (previews/scopes only).

## 4. Discrepancies with the owner's directive

1. **ISO 14001:2026** - the owner's year IS supported (published 15 Apr 2026 per DEKRA, SIS), so ISO 14001:2015 should NOT be treated as current. Caveat: no ISO primary page read.
2. **ISO 19011:2026** - the owner's year IS supported (EVS national adoption valid 15 Jun 2026; CQI May 2026). 2018 edition withdrawn. International publication date unverified from ISO.
3. **ISO 9001:2026** - confirmed published 16 Sep 2026 (ISO/TC 176 page). Any content built on ISO 9001:2015 or AS9100D (ISO 9001:2015-based) alignment must be flagged: AS9100D, AS9110C and AS9120B still reference ISO 9001:2015 and sit in a not-yet-resolved transition to IA9100.
4. **AS9100 Rev D / AS9110 Rev C / AS9120 Rev B (2016)** - confirmed as the current published SAE revisions for AS9100D and AS9110C; AS9120B read via search only. Directive "Rev C?" for AS9110 and "Rev B?" for AS9120 are consistent. **IA9100 (and IA9110/IA9120) have no verified publication date or transition period**; sources conflict (8 months, ~12 months, 13 months, 3 years).
5. **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 incl. Amd 1:2024** - amendment verified (ISO committee page). Base-edition currency not read from ISO's own page.
6. **IATF 16949** - owner's "2016 + sanctioned interpretations" is correct today, but a 2nd edition is officially in development (SC-2026-005); timeline UNVERIFIED.
7. **ISO 45001:2018** and **ISO 22000:2018** - 2018 + Amd 1:2024 are current, but both have revisions in DIS stage; neither published.
8. **ISO 13485:2016** - reconfirmed (per TUV Nord) - ISO primary not seen. Directive's "amendments" question: none confirmed here.
9. **AS9101** - directive gives no revision; current is **G (Nov 2022)**. **AS9104/1** - current is **/1A (Jan 2022)**.
10. Any item the directive dates by year only (14001, 19011) should be re-expressed with full designations and an "as of" date in the product.
