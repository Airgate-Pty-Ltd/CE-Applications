**Technical authority:** Conform-Edge. **Final technical approval for the initial AS9100D production pack:** the owner (Brian Olea).
**Status today: NOT VERIFIED.** All obligations are AI-assisted drafts. Nothing here is release-verified, and nothing may be marked verified except by the named human reviewers below.

| Item | Location |

## For each auditable obligation the record keeps
1. **Source edition and identity** — source identity, edition, and the identifier of the controlled copy used (`source`).
2. **Exact clause hierarchy** through the lettered/numbered subdivision — state `clauseHierarchy`. Reviewed against the controlled copy. Do not accept a designator the source does not organise that way.
3. **Independently authored interpretation** — state `interpretation` (intent, aspects, Should Be paraphrase).
4. **Intended outcome** — state `intendedOutcome`.
5. **Applicability conditions** — state `applicability` (with the authored conditions).
6. **Source-verification evidence** — `evidenceRef` on the clause state (e.g. dated copy-reference note).
7. **Interpretation-review evidence** — `evidenceRef` on the interpretation, outcome and applicability states.
8. **Reviewer identity, date, status** — `reviewer`, `reviewDate`, `result` on every state; and `finalApproval` {status, approver, date, evidenceRef}.

**Clause structure and substantive interpretation have separate states.** A verified clause identifier does not establish that the authored interpretation is correct, and vice versa.

## Rules the software enforces (release mode and production NCR gate)
- All four states must be `VERIFIED` with a reviewer, an ISO date and an evidence reference.
- Reviewers and the final approver must be accountable humans: names containing AI/assistant/model/bot terms are rejected.
- No self-approval: a reviewer or approver equal to `authoredBy` is rejected.
- Final approval must be `APPROVED` with approver and date.
- Source identity, edition and controlled-copy identifier are required.
- The record must match the pack version, source edition and each obligation's exact paragraph.
- Unresolved content cannot be marked release-verified by any code path; the shipped record has every state `PENDING` (tested).

## Procedure
1. Reviewer opens the worksheet with the **controlled, licensed copy**. Do not rely on recollection, model output, or third-party summaries.
2. Complete section A and B first (clause structure). Record outcome and evidence; amend `src/synthetic-data.js` / the index if the structure differs, then regenerate with `node tools/export-demo-pack.js`.
3. A different person (or the same human reviewer, never the AI) completes C–E (interpretation, outcome, applicability). Rejected items return to authoring; the record keeps the rejection.
4. The owner records final approval per obligation.
5. Run `node tools/validate-content.js packs/as9100d-demo.pack.json --index data/as9100d-clause-index.sample.json --mode release --verification-record data/source-verification-record.json`. The evidence JSON is retained with the pack version.
6. Only then may the pack's `source.verification.status` and the index status be set to VERIFIED by the human authority (not by code).

## Scope note
The demonstration pack has 5 obligations; the initial **production** pack is larger and is not yet authored to obligation grain. Authoring it is approved work; verifying it is the owner's technical-authority work.
