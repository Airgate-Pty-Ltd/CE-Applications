## Positioning
The gap in the market is *integrated maturity scoring across all five core tools plus IATF integration in one sitting*. ERP-QMS vendors score processes they host; point tools score one discipline; consultants score once. Nobody gives a Tier 2 quality manager a defensible 45-question maturity radar in 30 minutes for $199/mo.

## Credibility Risk (must fix)
OEM SQEs and IATF CB auditors now audit against the **AIAG-VDA FMEA Handbook (2019)** — Action Priority tables, 7-step process, FMEA-MSR. Our FMEA tab tests discipline but not methodology currency. A competitor (or a sharp prospect) noticing this undercuts the "built by an IATF lead auditor" claim. Fix is 3 added questions (see Compliance-Gap.md). Same logic applies to referencing current PPAP (4th ed.) and MSA (4th ed.) editions in report output.

## Moat
(a) Brian's IATF lead-auditor scoring judgment embedded in weightings; (b) quarterly content updates tracking CSR changes (Ford/GM/Stellantis/VW CSR documents change multiple times per year — this is the renewal engine no static checklist can match); (c) portfolio ecosystem (Supplier Scorecard for the customer's own supply base).

## Threat Watch
- eQMS vendors shipping "core tools assessment" templates (likely within 12 months) — counter with methodology currency + speed.
- AIAG itself digitizing assessments — partnership possibility rather than pure threat; monitor.
