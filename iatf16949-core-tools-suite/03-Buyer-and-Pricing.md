# Buyer Profile & Pricing Strategy

## Primary Buyer Personas

### Persona 1: The Tier 2 Quality Manager ("Katie")
- **Title**: Quality Manager, Supply Chain Manager
- **Company**: 75–500 employees, $20M–$200M revenue
- **Pain**: Managing 1–3 suppliers. Annual IATF certification due in 6 months. No consultant budget.
- **Wins if**: Passes IATF audit first visit (saves re-audit cost: $15k). Reduces prep time from 60 hrs to 6 hrs (saves $6k labor).
- **Decision timeline**: Fast (1–2 weeks); often makes own purchase decision under $1k/month.
- **Price sensitivity**: High. Will shop. Expects ROI in 3 months.
- **Volume**: ~8,000 in North America.

### Persona 2: The IATF Lead Auditor ("Michael")
- **Title**: Lead Auditor, Principal Auditor, Certification Manager
- **Company**: Certification body (TÜV, LRQA, BSI) OR consultant firm
- **Pain**: Clients arrive unprepared. Audits are inefficient. Post-audit CAR rate is high.
- **Wins if**: Recommends tool to clients → clients pass audits → lower rework / higher reputation.
- **Decision timeline**: Referral-based. Recommends to 20–50 clients/year.
- **Price sensitivity**: Low. Will recommend anything that helps clients.
- **Volume**: ~600 active IATF lead auditors globally; 200+ in North America.
- **Use case**: Recommends suite to clients before certification audit.

### Persona 3: The OEM Supplier Quality (Tier 1) ("David")
- **Title**: Director of Supplier Quality, VP of Supplier Engineering
- **Company**: Tier 1 supplier; 500–10,000 employees
- **Pain**: Managing 20–100 sub-tier suppliers. Need readiness visibility. Auditing them manually is expensive.
- **Wins if**: Dashboard shows which suppliers are at risk. Supports supplier training. Reduces supplier audit failures by 40%.
- **Decision timeline**: Slow (4–8 weeks). Needs cost-benefit case study. May pilot with 5 suppliers.
- **Price sensitivity**: Medium. $399–$699/mo is acceptable if ROI is proven.
- **Volume**: ~1,200 Tier 1 suppliers globally; 200+ in North America.
- **Use case**: Licenses tool for enterprise tier; manages supply base readiness.

### Persona 4: The Training Manager ("Sharon")
- **Title**: Training Manager, Competence Development Manager, Learning & Development
- **Company**: Any of above; handles corporate training
- **Pain**: Staff turnover. New Quality Engineers don't understand core tools. Need cost-effective training + assessment.
- **Wins if**: Tool doubles as training + assessment. Reduces training cost from $50k/year to $15k/year.
- **Decision timeline**: Annual budget cycle; or emergency reactive purchase.
- **Price sensitivity**: Low. Will buy if training ROI is proven.
- **Volume**: ~5,000 in manufacturing (all industries).

## Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Solo Supplier (Entry)
**$199/month or $1,990/year (save 15%)**

**Who**: Tier 2 suppliers, single-plant operations, <$100M revenue
- Unlimited users (same organization)
- 12 assessments/year
- All 45 questions (6 tabs)
- Customer-specific variant selection (Generic, Ford, GM, Stellantis)
- PDF report + CSV export
- Email support (48-hour response)
- Annual tool update (new IATF guidance, CSR variants)

**Value realization**: 
- Audit prep time saved: 60 hrs → 6 hrs (54 hrs × $150/hr = $8,100 value)
- Cost of failed IATF audit (re-audit fees, penalty production downtime): $15,000 risk mitigation
- ROI: ($8,100 + $15,000) / $199/mo = 46× ROI in Year 1

## Pricing Rationale

| Factor | Price Implication |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Cost to serve** | Software delivery: $100/mo (hosting, support). Gross margin: 70%+ on all tiers. |
| **Benchmark competitors** | SPC software: $500–$2,000/mo. FMEA tools: $200–$800/mo. Consulting: $150k–$300k flat. Our pricing: 70–90% below flat consulting, 50% below software-only competitors. |
| **Customer willingness to pay** | Tier 2 saves 54+ hrs/year at $150/hr labor = $8,100. Will easily justify $2,388/year ($199×12). |
| **Expansion revenue** | Tier 2 upsell to Tier 3 (group tools) = $3,990/year. Mid-tier to Enterprise = $6,990/year. |
| **Discount strategy** | Annual prepay discount (15% off): encourage long-term commitment + improve cash flow. |

## Go-To-Market Pricing Strategy

1. **Launch price**: $199 Solo tier. No discount (pay full; build confidence in value).
2. **First 50 customers**: Offer "Founding Customer" discount (20% off Year 1) + lifetime credit toward Tier 2 upgrade.
3. **Q1 2027**: Introduce Tier 2 + Tier 3.
4. **Q2 2027**: Annual prepay discount (15% off).
5. **Monitor churn**: If >15%/mo, lower price or add features. If <5%/mo and NPS >60, raise price.
