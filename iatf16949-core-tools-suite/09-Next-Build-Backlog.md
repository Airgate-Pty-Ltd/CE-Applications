## Backlog Voting & Prioritization Process

### Monthly (After Launch)
1. **Customer feedback** — aggregate feature requests from email/Gumroad comments
2. **Usage data** — track which tabs get most questions answered (signal importance)
3. **Competitive pressure** — monitor if competitors launch new features
4. **Revenue potential** — B2-3 (Dashboard) unlocks Tier 1 buyers, so prioritize early
5. **Effort vs. payoff** — B1-1 (free trial) is 6 hrs, unlocks 3× signups → do immediately

### Quarterly Planning
- Rank by: **Revenue Impact** × **User Demand** ÷ **Effort (hours)**
- Allocate 30% effort to "revenue blockers" (B1, B2)
- Allocate 30% to "vertical expansion" (B3)
- Allocate 20% to "AI/intelligence" (B4)
- Allocate 20% to "polish/retention" (B6)

### Hard Stop Condition (Revisit if)
- **Churn > 15%/mo**: Stop new features, focus on retention
- **Competitor launches superior feature**: Re-prioritize to match
- **Platform change** (e.g., browser deprecates localStorage): Emergency fix
