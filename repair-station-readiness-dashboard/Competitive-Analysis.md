## Positioning

**Category of one:** No product combines (1) regulatory surveillance posture, (2) QMS maturity scoring (AS9110C + 145.163 training), and (3) supplier quality health in a single risk-scored view for repair stations. Competitors do operations (MRO ERP), airworthiness (tracking tools), or generic quality (eQMS) — none do *readiness*.

**Honest weakness:** Incumbents have real data integrations today; our live-feed story is roadmap. Until v1.1, we win on scoring methodology, price, and speed-to-value — not on data plumbing. Sales copy must not claim live APIs (corrected 2026-07-07).

**Moat:** (a) Scoring methodology encodes a working AS9110C/Part 145/EASA lead auditor's judgment; (b) Supplier Scorecard v2.0 ecosystem integration; (c) M-AI Kernel governance layer once integrated — auditable rules-based intelligence that enterprise quality directors can defend to a regulator, which black-box AI competitors cannot.

## Threat Watch

- MRO ERP vendors adding a "compliance score" widget (12–18 month risk) — counter by owning the methodology narrative and the consultant channel now.
- AI-native startups wrapping LLMs around Part 145 (already appearing) — counter with governed, deterministic scoring + Brian's authority; regulated buyers distrust hallucination risk.
