## Highest-Value Expansion Paths (ranked)

1. **Scorecard + Dashboard Bundle ($179/mo, "Compliance Suite")** — The Supplier Scorecard v2.0 feeds dimension 3 of this product. A bundle at $179/mo (vs. $198 separately) raises ARPU ~2.4x over Basic and locks in two products' switching costs. *Strongest cross-sell in the portfolio; do this first.*
2. **Consultant / White-Label Edition ($499/mo per consultant, unlimited client stations)** — Brian's own consulting peers are a distribution channel. 20 consultants = $120k ARR with near-zero CAC. Also produces case studies.
3. **Enterprise Multi-Station ($500–$2,000/mo)** — Holding companies with 5–30 stations. 10 accounts at $1k/mo = $120k ARR. Gate: v1.2 consolidated view.
4. **Annual Prepay (2 months free)** — Standard SaaS cash-flow lever; target 40% annual mix by month 6.
5. **Readiness Report as a Product ($299 one-time)** — Single-shot "board-ready compliance posture report" for stations that won't subscribe yet; converts to subscription later. Low effort (print view already planned).
6. **Training Attach (Annex Quality)** — Alert categories map to training gaps (145.163). Sell HF/recurrent training packages through the dashboard's recommendations. Synergy with the Human Factors curriculum completed 2026-07-05.
7. **Government/Authority Edition (2027)** — FSDO-style oversight view; long sales cycle, park until product is proven.
