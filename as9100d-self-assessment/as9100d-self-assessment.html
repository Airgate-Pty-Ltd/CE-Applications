CL 4 Context of the Organization — ▶
Q1.The organization has determined internal and external issues relevant to its purpose and the QMS, including those relevant to aerospace customers and regulatory bodies.
AS9100D §4.1 | Context & Issues
Q2.Relevant interested parties (customers, regulatory authorities, certification bodies, OEMs, suppliers) and their requirements have been identified and documented.
AS9100D §4.2 | Interested Parties
Q3.The QMS scope is defined, documented, and includes applicable product/service lines, sites, and exclusions with justification (no exclusions allowed in clauses 8.3, 8.4).
AS9100D §4.3 | Scope of the QMS
CL 5 Leadership & Commitment — ▶
Q4.Top management demonstrates active leadership accountability for QMS effectiveness — not just delegation to Quality. Evidence includes visible involvement in management review, policy setting, and resource allocation.
AS9100D §5.1 | Leadership & Commitment
Q5.A quality policy exists that is appropriate to the organization, includes a commitment to comply with requirements and continually improve, is communicated, understood, and reviewed for continued suitability.
AS9100D §5.2 | Quality Policy
Q6.Organizational roles, responsibilities, and authorities are assigned and communicated. A Management Representative (or equivalent function) has been designated with specific QMS authority, including customer focus.
AS9100D §5.3 | Roles & Responsibilities
CL 6 Planning — ▶
Q7.Risks and opportunities are determined, assessed, and addressed — with specific consideration of operational risks and opportunities in the aerospace context (supply chain, regulatory changes, safety events).
AS9100D §6.1 | Risk & Opportunity Management
Q8.Quality objectives are established, measurable, aligned to policy, communicated, monitored, and include aerospace-specific metrics (OTD, first-pass yield, NCR rate, customer complaints, etc.).
AS9100D §6.2 | Quality Objectives
CL 7 Support (Resources, Competence, Communication, Documented Information) — ▶
Q9.Organizational knowledge needed for QMS operation has been determined, maintained, and made available. Lessons learned from nonconformities, customer feedback, and project reviews are captured and applied.
AS9100D §7.1.6 | Organizational Knowledge
Q10.Personnel performing QMS-affecting work are competent — education, training, skills, and experience are defined, verified, and recorded. Training effectiveness is evaluated.
AS9100D §7.2 | Competence
Q11.Documented information required by AS9100D is created, controlled, and maintained. Document control includes approval, version management, distribution control, and prevention of unintended use of obsolete documents.
AS9100D §7.5 | Documented Information
CL 8 Operation — Planning, Customer Requirements & Aerospace-Specific Controls — ▶
Q12.Operational planning defines quality criteria, resources, control of externally provided items, required monitoring/measurement points, acceptance criteria, and records of conformance for each product/service line.
AS9100D §8.1 | Operational Planning & Control
Q13.Configuration management is established — covering product/part identification, change control, configuration status accounting, and configuration audits. Engineering change management is documented and controlled.
AS9100D §8.1.2 | Configuration Management (Aerospace-Specific)
Q14.Product safety is proactively managed — including key characteristics identification, Safety Management practices, escapes reporting, and awareness of the consequences of failures for safety-critical products.
AS9100D §8.1.3 | Product Safety (Aerospace-Specific)
Q15.Customer requirements are determined and reviewed before acceptance (including airworthiness requirements, regulatory/statutory requirements, and product safety requirements). Records of reviews are maintained.
AS9100D §8.2 | Customer Requirements Review
Q16.Design and development planning, inputs, controls, outputs, and changes are managed. Design reviews, verification, and validation are performed. Design changes are controlled and approved before implementation.
AS9100D §8.3 | Design & Development
Q17.Externally provided processes, products, and services are controlled. Supplier selection, evaluation, re-evaluation, and performance monitoring are conducted. Approved Supplier Lists (ASL) are maintained and current.
AS9100D §8.4 | Control of Externally Provided Processes
Q18.Counterfeit part prevention measures are in place — including supplier controls, receiving inspection for suspect counterfeit parts, segregation, and reporting. Awareness training is conducted for relevant personnel.
AS9100D §8.1.4 | Prevention of Counterfeit Parts (Aerospace-Specific)
Q19.Special processes (welding, heat treat, NDT, plating, composites, etc.) are validated and controlled. Personnel performing special processes are qualified/certified. Records of qualification are maintained.
AS9100D §8.5.1 | Special Processes & Process Validation
Q20.First Article Inspection (FAI) is performed in accordance with AS9102 (or equivalent customer requirement) for new parts, changes to design/process/supplier, and production interruptions meeting defined thresholds.
AS9100D §8.5.1 / First Article Inspection (AS9102 linkage)
Q21.Key Characteristics (KCs) are identified, flowed down from customer requirements, controlled through production, and monitored with statistical or other methods. KC results are recorded and traceability is maintained.
AS9100D §8.5.1 | Key Characteristics
Q22.Product identification and traceability is maintained throughout production — from receiving through shipment. Lot/serial/batch traceability can be reconstructed to raw material, process, and inspection records.
AS9100D §8.5.2 | Identification & Traceability
Q23.Release of products/services is controlled — acceptance criteria are defined, inspection/test activities are performed and documented, and conformance is authorized before release. Authorized release documents are issued correctly.
AS9100D §8.6 | Release of Products & Services
Q24.Nonconforming outputs are identified, controlled, and segregated. Disposition (use-as-is, rework, scrap, return to supplier) is reviewed and authorized. The customer and/or regulatory authority are notified when required.
AS9100D §8.7 | Control of Nonconforming Outputs
CL 9 Performance Evaluation — ▶
Q25.Customer satisfaction is monitored — including customer complaint data, on-time delivery, quality escapes to customer, and any customer scorecards. Trends are analyzed and acted upon.
AS9100D §9.1.2 | Customer Satisfaction
Q26.Data analysis covers: customer satisfaction, conformance to requirements, process and product characteristics, supplier performance, and QMS trend analysis. Results are used to support management review and continual improvement decisions.
AS9100D §9.1.3 | Analysis & Evaluation
Q27.Internal audits are planned and conducted systematically. The audit program covers all processes and clauses. Auditors are objective and impartial. Findings are reported and corrective actions are verified. Audit records are maintained.
AS9100D §9.2 | Internal Audit
Q28.Management review is conducted at planned intervals with all required AS9100D inputs (including operational KPIs, audit results, customer feedback, NC/CAR status, risk status). Outputs include decisions and action items with owners and due dates.
AS9100D §9.3 | Management Review
CL 10 Improvement (Corrective Action, NCR/CAR, Continual Improvement) — ▶
Q29.Nonconformities are documented in a formal NCR/CAR system. Root cause analysis is performed using structured methodologies (5-Why, Fishbone, 8D). Corrective actions address root cause, not symptom. Effectiveness verification is performed.
AS9100D §10.2 | Nonconformity & Corrective Action
Q30.The organization systematically identifies opportunities for improvement — beyond corrective action. Improvement projects are linked to quality objectives, KPIs, and customer satisfaction data. Results are tracked and reported.
AS9100D §10.3 | Continual Improvement
AE+ Aerospace-Specific Additional Requirements (Customer-Specific & OASIS) — ▶
Q31.Customer-specific requirements (CSRs) — including OEM Quality Requirements (Boeing D6-82479, Airbus AIPI 00-00-004, etc.) — have been reviewed, captured, and flowed down to applicable processes and suppliers.
AS9100D §8.2 / Customer-Specific Requirements
Q32.The organization is registered in OASIS (or applicable customer portals) and the registration is current and accurate. Certificate of conformance data is available and accessible to customers as required.
IAQG / OASIS Registration Requirements
Q33.Measurement and test equipment is calibrated, controlled, and protected against damage. Calibration records include equipment ID, calibration status, calibration date, next due date, and calibration standard traceability to national standards.
AS9100D §7.1.5 | Monitoring & Measuring Resources
Q34.Preservation of products — including handling, storage, packaging, and protection — is defined and implemented. Environmental controls (temperature, humidity, ESD, shelf life) are applied as required for aerospace materials and assemblies.
AS9100D §8.5.4 | Preservation
Q35.Post-delivery support obligations are defined — including warranty processes, field returns, escape notification to customers, and handling of post-delivery nonconformities. If not in scope, this is documented as justified.
AS9100D §8.5.5 | Post-Delivery Activities
