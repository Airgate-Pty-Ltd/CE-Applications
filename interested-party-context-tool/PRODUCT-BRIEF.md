# Product Brief — Interested Party & Context Analysis Tool
**Brand:** Conform-Edge Quality Consulting LLC
**Route:** Software
**Status:** PROTOTYPE — launch blockers remain

## What It Is
Single-file HTML tool for documenting and self-assessing Clause 4.1 (context of the organization) and 4.2 (interested parties and their requirements). Dynamic party registry, completeness engine, 3-tier verdict, MR narrative, JSON export.

## Target Buyer
Quality managers at ISO 9001 or AS9100D-registered organizations preparing for CB certification, surveillance audits, or management reviews. Secondary: consultants building QMS foundations for new clients.

## Problem Solved
4.1/4.2 is frequently cited by CB auditors as weak — organizations document it once and never revisit. This tool makes the review structured, repeatable, and MR-ready.
