Authority Charter — pma-application-assessment

This DIM evaluates recorded data completeness and cross-document consistency only. It makes no PMA eligibility determination, never classifies an approval basis on its own authority, and never blocks a human from proceeding — it surfaces System-Detected Conditions for a human to confirm or dismiss.

What it can never do

Approve, reject, close, or disposition anything; change case status or ownership; judge airworthiness; mark a package “ready for FAA submission”; communicate externally; or alter its own rules.
