### Situation
You have a quality problem. Defect rates are too high. Delivery times are slipping. Customer complaints are mounting.

### Agitation
You try to fix it, but where do you even start? Your team works in silos—some collecting data, some guessing at root causes, some testing untested ideas. Without structure, you waste months and money on fixes that don't stick. Then the old problem comes back.

### Solution
DMAIC gives you a proven playbook. Define the problem. Measure what's actually happening. Analyze the root cause (not your gut feeling). Improve with a pilot. Control the new process so it stays fixed.

Our tool makes each step clear, fast, and data-driven. No more guessing. No more wasted effort.

**Result:** Most organizations see 20-40% improvement in their targeted metric within 8-12 weeks. And it stays improved.
