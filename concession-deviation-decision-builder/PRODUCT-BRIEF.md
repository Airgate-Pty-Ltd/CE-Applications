# Concession & Deviation Decision Builder
**Brand:** Conform-Edge Quality Consulting  
**Category:** Software / HTML Decision Tool + Record Generator  
**Status:** Publish-ready (verified 2026-08-15)

## Problem
Every QMS organization deals with nonconforming product and planned departures, yet the decision path — concession vs. deviation, approval authority, customer notification triggers, regulatory requirements — is inconsistent, verbal, or poorly documented. Wrong dispositions on KC/safety characteristics at aerospace companies are audit findings and regulatory violations.

## Solution
A browser-based deterministic tool that:
1. Classifies the departure as Concession (after-production) or Deviation/Production Permit (before production)
2. Applies a 3-gate engine to identify hard stops, required approval authority, and mandatory notifications
3. Generates a formatted request record with signature blocks ready for QMS entry

Covers: AS9100D 8.7, ISO 9001:2015 8.7, IATF 16949, ISO 13485, with aviation (FAA/EASA) and medical device (FDA QMSR) overlays.
