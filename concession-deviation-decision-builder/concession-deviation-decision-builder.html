Concession & Deviation Decision Builder

Conform-Edge · AS9100 8.7 Deterministic Gate
Self-contained. No data leaves this page.

Determine whether a nonconforming product or planned departure requires a concession (accept nonconforming product after the fact) or deviation / production permit (pre-approved departure before production). The engine evaluates eligibility, required approval authority, and mandatory customer notification under AS9100D clause 8.7 / ISO 9001 clause 8.7 and equivalent IATF 16949 and ISO 13485 requirements. Generates a request record ready for review.

Reference: AS9100D clause 8.7 and ISO 9001:2015 clause 8.7 by clause intent only. Regulatory requirements (FAA, EASA, FDA) cited by intent — verify current regulations before submission. This tool generates a draft decision and request record; final approval authority rests with the responsible engineering and quality management personnel.
