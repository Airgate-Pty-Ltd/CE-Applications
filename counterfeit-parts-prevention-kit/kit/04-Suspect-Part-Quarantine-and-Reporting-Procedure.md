# Suspect Counterfeit Part — Quarantine and Reporting Procedure
**[The Organization]** · Doc ID: [PLACEHOLDER]

## 1. Immediate Actions (same day)
1. Tag part(s) "SUSPECT COUNTERFEIT — DO NOT USE" and move to controlled
   quarantine. Physical + system quarantine (block stock location).
2. Impound all documentation, packaging, and related lot stock.
3. Notify QM and Purchasing. Freeze payment where possible.

## 2. Investigation
- Verify traceability chain with claimed sources directly (not via the seller).
- Perform/commission verification per checklist Level 2/3.
- Disposition by MRB: confirmed counterfeit / cleared / indeterminate
  (indeterminate is controlled as counterfeit).

## 3. Reporting (confirmed or high-confidence suspect)
| Recipient | When required | Record |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Customer(s) affected | Per contract/flow-down | Notification record |
| GIDEP or equivalent reporting program | Where contractually/legally required [PLACEHOLDER: confirm applicability] | Report copy |
| Supplier | After evidence secured — never before | Correspondence |
| Authorities / legal | [PLACEHOLDER: per counsel and contract] | As advised |

## 4. Final Disposition
Confirmed counterfeit parts are physically destroyed or rendered unusable and
retained/disposed per customer direction; never returned for credit, never
scrapped recoverable, never resold.

## 5. Corrective Action
Enter the escape into the corrective action system; assess sourcing decision
tree compliance and detection effectiveness; update risk profile and training.
