## 1. Purpose and Scope
Prevent the purchase, acceptance, installation, and delivery of counterfeit or
suspect counterfeit parts and materiel. Applies to: [PLACEHOLDER: commodities,
programs, sites].

## 2. Definitions
Counterfeit part: an unauthorized copy, imitation, substitute, or modified part
misrepresented as a specified genuine part. Suspect part: a part for which there
is objective indication it may be counterfeit. Authorized source: OCM/OEM or
their franchised/authorized distribution chain.

## 3. Program Elements
### 3.1 Risk-Based Sourcing (see Decision Tree, doc 02)
Parts are procured from authorized sources unless unavailable. Non-authorized
procurement requires documented risk assessment and escalated verification,
approved by [PLACEHOLDER: role].

### 3.2 Procurement Flow-Down
Purchase orders to non-OCM sources include the flow-down clauses in doc 05
(traceability, certification, right of return, notification of suspect parts).

### 3.3 Verification and Detection
Receiving inspection applies the checklist in doc 03, scaled to sourcing risk:
Level 1 (authorized, full trace) documentation review; Level 2 adds enhanced
visual criteria; Level 3 (broker/independent) adds [PLACEHOLDER: e.g., external
test lab verification per contract or risk assessment].

### 3.4 Control of Suspect Parts
Suspect parts are immediately quarantined per doc 04. Suspect parts are never
returned to the supplier for credit before disposition, never scrapped in a
recoverable state, and never reintroduced to stock.

### 3.5 Reporting
Confirmed counterfeit occurrences are reported per doc 04 to: customer(s),
the reporting programs applicable by contract (e.g., GIDEP where required),
and [PLACEHOLDER: authorities where contractually/legally required].

### 3.6 Training
Personnel in procurement, receiving inspection, and stores complete counterfeit
awareness training per doc 06 at hire and every [PLACEHOLDER] months.

### 3.7 Records
Sourcing risk assessments, verification records, quarantine and disposition
records, reports, and training records are retained per [PLACEHOLDER].
