# Supplier Flow-Down Clauses — Counterfeit Prevention
**Insert into POs / supplier quality requirements. [PLACEHOLDER: legal review
before first use.]**

1. **Authorized sourcing.** Seller shall supply only parts procured from the
   OCM/OEM or its authorized distribution chain, unless Buyer has approved an
   alternate source in writing prior to shipment.
2. **Traceability.** Seller shall deliver documentation establishing the
   complete supply chain from OCM to Seller for each shipment, and shall retain
   supporting records for [PLACEHOLDER] years, available on request.
3. **Certification.** Seller certifies parts delivered are genuine, unused, and
   conform to the specified part number and revision.
4. **Notification.** Seller shall notify Buyer within [PLACEHOLDER] days of any
   indication that parts delivered are or may be counterfeit.
5. **Remedies.** Counterfeit or suspect parts are nonconforming; Buyer may
   withhold payment, and parts shall not be returned for credit. Seller bears
   replacement and verification costs.
6. **Flow-down.** Seller shall flow these requirements to its sub-tier sources.
7. **Counterfeit prevention program.** Seller shall maintain a counterfeit
   prevention process consistent with industry practice (AS5553/AS6174 intent)
   proportionate to the commodity supplied.
