# Receiving Verification & Detection Checklist
**Scale to verification level from the sourcing decision tree. Retain completed
checklist as a quality record.**

## Level 1 — Documentation (all receipts)
- [ ] CoC/ATA-106/8130-3 (as applicable) present, consistent, unaltered
- [ ] Part number, revision, quantity match PO and markings
- [ ] Traceability chain complete to claimed source
- [ ] Date/lot codes plausible and internally consistent

## Level 2 — Enhanced Visual (add for limited-trace sources)
- [ ] Packaging: correct OCM labeling, no evidence of re-labeling/re-taping
- [ ] Markings: font, depth, alignment, spelling; no ghost markings under paint
- [ ] Surfaces: sanding/blacktopping indicators, resurfacing, solder reflow
- [ ] Leads/connectors: re-tinning, oxidation inconsistent with claimed date
- [ ] Country-of-origin consistency across part, label, and docs
- [ ] Compare against known-good exemplar or OCM datasheet imagery

## Level 3 — External/Instrumented (broker/independent buys)
- [ ] [PLACEHOLDER: e.g., decapsulation, XRF, X-ray, electrical test per risk
      assessment or contract — specify lab and scope]
- [ ] Test report reviewed and attached

## Disposition
- [ ] PASS → accept to stock, record retained
- [ ] SUSPECT → quarantine immediately per doc 04 — do NOT return to supplier
