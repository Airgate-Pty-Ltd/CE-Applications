# Product Brief — counterfeit-parts-prevention-kit

## Product Name
Counterfeit Parts Prevention Program Kit (AS5553 / AS6174 aligned)

## Product Category
QMS Kit / Audit Kit (Annex Quality hard-copy route)

## Buyer
Quality Managers at aerospace/defense manufacturers, distributors, and repair
stations facing AS9100D counterfeit-part prevention requirements, customer
flow-downs (e.g., DFARS counterfeit electronic part clauses), or CB audit
findings on clause-level counterfeit prevention.

## Pain
AS9100D requires planned counterfeit-part prevention processes; primes flow down
AS5553 (electronic parts) / AS6174 (materiel) expectations; DFARS exposes
contractors to unallowable-cost and reporting obligations. Most SMEs have a
one-paragraph procedure that fails intent-based audits: no risk-based sourcing
control, no detection criteria, no quarantine/reporting flow, no training
evidence. Repeat CB finding territory.

## Decision Supported
"Is our counterfeit prevention program defensible to our CB, our primes, and
DFARS — and if not, which specific controls and records must we add?"

## Data Required
Commodity risk profile, supply chain map (OCM/authorized/independent
distributors), procurement flow-downs, inspection capability, past escapes.

## Evidence Produced
Program procedure, risk-based sourcing decision tree records, inspection/
detection checklist records, quarantine + reporting records (GIDEP/customer),
training records, supplier flow-down text.

## Recommendation Logic
Self-assessment checklist rates each control area compliant/partial/missing →
maps each gap to the exact procedure section and record that closes it.

## Business Outcome
Closes CB findings, passes prime supplier audits, reduces escape and
unallowable-cost risk.

## Why This Is Different
Written to auditor evidence expectations (what a CB samples), intent-based, and
scoped for SMEs — not a 200-page prime-contractor manual. Ledger check: no
counterfeit-parts product exists in the portfolio despite as9100d-auditor and
as9120b-auditor skills both flagging it — direct IP reuse.

## Pricing Hypothesis
$399 kit. 650 sales/yr (aerospace SME + distributor market, plus AS9120
stockists) = $259,350/yr. Upsell: AQ training micro-course; distributor variant.

## First-Dollar Path
LinkedIn post on counterfeit findings; direct offer to AS9120 distributor
contacts (Ontic/Sugesh thread in ledger); Gumroad listing.

## Reuse / IP Captured
Counterfeit control decomposition reusable in as9100d/as9120b auditor skills,
a future Conform-Edge module, and an Annex Quality course.
