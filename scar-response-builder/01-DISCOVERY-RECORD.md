## Gateway Questions

**1. Who is the exact buyer and what triggers the purchase?**
Quality engineer or quality manager at an aerospace, automotive, or medical device supplier who has received a Supplier Corrective Action Request (SCAR) from a customer (Boeing, Lockheed, Honeywell, a Tier-1 prime, or an OEM) and must submit a structured response within a defined timeframe (typically 24–72 hrs for D1–D3, 30 days for D4–D8). Trigger: SCAR received; customer threatens dock-and-hold or supplier disqualification; internal team has never done a formal 8D response.

**2. What outcome does the buyer walk away with?**
A complete, structured SCAR response package: D1 team definition, D2 problem description (IS/IS-NOT), D3 containment, D4 root cause (5-Why + Fishbone prompts), D5 corrective actions, D6 implementation verification, D7 systemic prevention, D8 team recognition + closure evidence checklist. Formatted for download as the customer's preferred format (generic 8D table). Escape-point analysis prompt included. Customer-facing narrative tone.

**3. Why does this earn $250k+ per year?**
Every aerospace, automotive, and medical device supplier receives SCARs. ~40,000+ active Tier 1/2 suppliers across these three sectors. At $149–$199 one-time (single use is fine — they buy again for the next SCAR), or $299/yr unlimited: 1,500 units/yr = $250k path. Distinct from the QMS-manager 8D tool; complementary portfolio item creating a SCAR ecosystem.

**4. Is this buildable as a self-contained HTML tool?**
Yes. SCAR response follows a deterministic 8D structure. Each discipline has guided input fields with prompts, validation, and output formatting. No external deps. JSON export for record-keeping.

**5. Does this conflict with anything in the portfolio?**
Distinct from 8D Corrective Action HTML Workflow Tool (07-01) which processes findings from the QMS manager's side. This is the supplier receiving a SCAR from a customer — different workflow, different user, different moment of pain. Complementary, not duplicative.
