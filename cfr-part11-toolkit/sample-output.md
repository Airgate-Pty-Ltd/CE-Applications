This is a critical gap if audited.

## Prioritized Remediation List

The following actions are listed in recommended execution order based on audit risk and implementation effort:

1. **Confirm or re-send FDA Part 11 certification letter** (Q29) — Low effort; legal/regulatory task; immediate action
2. **Execute overdue audit trail review** (Q22) — Medium effort; must be done before any FDA inspection
3. **Validate biometric e-signature system** (Q36) — High effort; requires validation protocol, OQ/PQ execution
4. **Assess SaaS vendors for DI controls** (Q47) — Medium effort; add to supplier qualification procedure; complete 3 assessments
5. **Add software/SaaS vendor category to supplier qualification SOP** (Q46) — Low effort; SOP update
6. **Document DI Officer role in a formal job description or procedure** (Q2) — Low effort; HR/QA task
7. **Document speak-up / non-retaliation procedure** (Q7) — Low effort; add section to DI policy or create standalone SOP
8. **Implement system clock synchronization control and document it** (Q27) — Low-medium effort; IT and QA task
9. **Reconcile paper form issuance log; control blank forms** (Q18) — Medium effort; requires process change and training
10. **Resolve LIMS/DHR date-time consistency issue** (Q15) — Medium-high effort; root cause investigation required
