## Primary Buyer Personas

### Persona 1: The QA/RA Manager at a Medical Device OEM
**Name:** Sarah M., Quality Systems Manager
**Company:** Mid-size US medical device manufacturer, $25M–$150M revenue, 80–400 employees
**Regulated products:** Class II devices (510(k) cleared), ISO 13485 certified, 21 CFR 820 / QMSR subject

**Pain points:**
- Upcoming FDA inspection or notified body audit in 3–9 months
- Just read a Warning Letter issued to a competitor citing data integrity deficiencies
- No budget for a $10,000 consulting engagement but needs a gap baseline
- QMS software (LIMS, eDHR, document control) was set up years ago and never formally validated under Part 11

**Buying trigger:** FDA inspection scheduled, competitor Warning Letter published, or internal audit finding citing "audit trail not reviewed"

**Decision authority:** Approves purchases under $1,000 independently; presents $500–$2,000 purchases to VP Quality

**How they find this product:** LinkedIn post, Regulatory Focus newsletter, Google search "21 CFR Part 11 gap assessment tool", Conform-Edge.com

**What makes them buy:** Clear regulatory references in the questions, demo mode that shows exactly what they'll get, printable report they can put in an inspection binder, and a price below $500
