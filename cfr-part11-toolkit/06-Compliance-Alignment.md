## FDA Guidance Documents Referenced

1. **21 CFR Part 11, Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures — Final Rule (1997)**
   Codified at 21 CFR Part 11

2. **FDA Guidance for Industry: Part 11, Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures — Scope and Application (August 2003)**
   Narrows enforcement to systems where Part 11 applies; clarifies validation expectations

3. **FDA Data Integrity and Compliance With Drug CGMP — Questions and Answers (December 2018)**
   While nominally pharma-focused, FDA has applied these ALCOA++ principles to device manufacturers under QMSR (2026)

4. **General Principles of Software Validation — Final Guidance (January 2002)**
   The primary CSV guidance; defines IQ/OQ/PQ; cited in Questions Q44, Q45, Q49, Q50

5. **GAMP 5: A Risk-Based Approach to Compliant GxP Computerized Systems (ISPE, 2nd Ed. 2022)**
   Industry best practice for CSV; cited for cloud/SaaS controls in Q47

6. **21 CFR Part 820 — Quality System Regulation (QMSR), effective February 2, 2026**
   Replaces legacy QSR; aligns with ISO 13485:2016; incorporates ALCOA++ expectations
