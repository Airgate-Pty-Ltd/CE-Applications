## Market Validation

### Regulatory Driver
21 CFR Part 11 (1997, reaffirmed 2003 FDA Guidance) governs all electronic records and electronic signatures in FDA-regulated activities. The QMSR final rule (21 CFR Part 820 refresh, effective February 2, 2026) explicitly aligns with ISO 13485:2016 and incorporates ALCOA++ data integrity expectations into US medical device QMS requirements for the first time at this level of specificity.

This creates a **compliance cliff**: thousands of device manufacturers must now demonstrate ALCOA++ compliance in their DHR/DHF records, not just pharmaceutical GMP compliance. The retrofit burden is significant and most QA teams have no structured assessment tool.
