## Product Boundaries — What This Tool Is and Is Not

### What This Tool IS
- A structured self-assessment aid for FDA-regulated organizations preparing for data integrity audits or inspections
- A gap identification and prioritization tool based on 21 CFR Part 11 and ALCOA++ principles
- A training and awareness resource for quality teams learning Part 11 requirements
- A documentation artifact suitable for inclusion in an inspection readiness binder (as evidence of proactive gap assessment)
- A starting point for building a CAPA list targeting data integrity controls

### What This Tool IS NOT
- A formal compliance certification or audit credential
- A substitute for a qualified regulatory affairs professional or FDA compliance consultant
- A guarantee of FDA inspection approval or warning letter immunity
- A legal opinion on whether a specific system, practice, or record qualifies as an "electronic record" under 21 CFR Part 11
- A validated computer system under 21 CFR Part 11 (the tool itself is not a GxP system; using it does not create GxP records)
- An FDA-recognized third-party assessment scheme (no such scheme exists for Part 11)
- A substitute for a formal computer system validation (CSV) of any specific system

## Regulatory Scope Assumptions

### ALCOA++ — No Official Certification Exists
"ALCOA++" is an FDA-articulated set of data integrity principles derived from GMP/GLP/GCP guidance documents. There is no FDA-issued ALCOA++ certification, compliance certificate, or recognized third-party accreditation body. This tool assesses alignment with the principles; it does not confer any official status.

### 21 CFR Part 11 — Scope Interpretation Required
FDA's August 2003 Guidance significantly narrowed the scope of Part 11 enforcement. Whether a given system or record is subject to Part 11 depends on the specific predicate rule, the nature of the record, and the system's function. Questions in this tool are written to reflect the general intent of Part 11 requirements; organizations must make their own Part 11 applicability determinations with qualified counsel.

### QMSR (21 CFR Part 820) — Effective February 2, 2026
Questions reference the QMSR final rule as effective. Organizations still operating under transition may have different current requirements; verify your effective compliance date.

### Pharmaceutical Applicability
The tool is designed primarily for medical device manufacturers under QMSR. Pharmaceutical manufacturers under 21 CFR Parts 210/211 face similar but not identical ALCOA++ and Part 11 requirements. Pharmaceutical users should verify question relevance against their applicable predicate rules (cGMP, GCP, GLP).

## Scoring Assumptions and Limitations

### Scores Are Self-Reported
All scores reflect the assessor's honest self-assessment. The tool cannot verify whether the controls described actually exist or function as described. A score of 5 ("Fully compliant") does not mean an FDA inspector will agree.

### Single-Assessor Bias
A single QA Manager may rate controls higher than a blind audit would find. Best practice: have two independent reviewers complete the assessment separately, then reconcile differences as a team discussion.

### Snapshot in Time
Assessment results reflect the organization's posture at the time of assessment. Regulatory requirements, organizational changes, and system changes can alter the score. Re-assess at minimum annually, and after any:
- Significant change to a computerized system
- Data integrity deviation or CAPA finding
- New FDA guidance publication
- Warning Letter issuance in your product category

### Partial Completion
Section scores calculated from partially answered sections reflect only the answered questions. If some questions are skipped, section scores may overstate compliance (if the skipped questions would have scored low).

## Legal Boundaries

The footer disclaimer on every page of the application states:

> "This tool is a self-assessment aid for audit preparation. Results do not guarantee FDA inspection approval. Consult qualified regulatory affairs professionals for formal compliance determinations. 21 CFR Part 11 cited by requirement intent only."

This disclaimer is legally important and must not be removed. It establishes that:
1. The tool output is not professional regulatory advice
2. Conform-Edge does not warrant regulatory outcomes
3. Users are responsible for their own compliance determinations
