### Sub-Headline

### Feature Bullets

**1. 50 expert-curated questions across every Part 11 and ALCOA++ dimension**
Every question is mapped to a specific 21 CFR Part 11 section (Subpart B electronic records, Subpart C electronic signatures) or ALCOA++ attribute (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available). No generic checklists. Each question is written by someone who has sat across from an FDA investigator.

**2. Instant maturity score with visual radar chart**
Your organization's score appears in real time as you answer each question — by section and overall. The six-axis radar chart shows exactly where you're strong and where you're exposed. Green means you're covered. Red means fix it before the inspection.

**3. Printable gap report ready for your inspection binder**
Click "Generate Report" and your browser produces a print-formatted gap report with your organization's name, assessment date, section scores, priority ratings, and a drill-down of every question scored 1–2 (critical gaps). Export to CSV for direct import into your CAPA system. No subscription required for the one-time license.
