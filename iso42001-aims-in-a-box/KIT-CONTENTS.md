A productized, instant-download AI Management System kit for organizations that build, provide, or use AI.
Authored intent-based and plain-language (Conform-Edge house style). Buyer tailors bracketed placeholders.

resolved; all files, headers, footers, and marketing copy in this folder use Conform-Edge branding only.
