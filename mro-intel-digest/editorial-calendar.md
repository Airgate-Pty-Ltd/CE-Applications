## SOURCES TO CHECK EACH QUARTER

### FAA
- [ ] FAA NPRM tracker: regulations.gov (search "FAA Part 145")
- [ ] FAA Advisory Circular updates: faa.gov/regulations_policies/advisory_circulars
- [ ] FAA Order updates (8900 series for repair stations)
- [ ] FAA FSIMS policy updates
- [ ] FlightAware / AOPA / Aviation Week for industry interpretations

### EASA
- [ ] EASA Rulemaking updates: easa.europa.eu/document-library/rulemaking-decisions
- [ ] EASA NPA/CRD releases
- [ ] MAG change announcements (US-EU bilateral)
- [ ] Safety Information Bulletins relevant to Part-145
- [ ] EASA Annual Safety Review

### UK CAA
- [ ] CAA CAPs (Civil Aviation Publications): caa.co.uk/cap-library
- [ ] UK Statutory Instruments (aviation safety): legislation.gov.uk
- [ ] CAA Enforcement actions quarterly bulletin
- [ ] UK CAA Airworthiness Notices

### CAAC
- [ ] CAAC regulatory notices: caac.gov.cn (EN and CN sections)
- [ ] CCAR amendment notices
- [ ] CAAC AC series updates (AC-145-XX)
- [ ] China Airport & Aviation News (industry layer)

### CASA
- [ ] CASA legislative instruments: legislation.gov.au
- [ ] CASA Advisory Circulars: casa.gov.au/advisory-circulars
- [ ] CASA Exemptions relevant to Part 145
- [ ] CASA Airworthiness Directives

## WORKFLOW CHECKLIST

### 6 weeks before publish
- [ ] Review all source sites for the quarter
- [ ] Fill authority monitoring log
- [ ] Identify top 2–3 items per authority

### 3 weeks before publish
- [ ] Draft all section content
- [ ] Complete compliance calendar
- [ ] Complete cross-authority radar table
- [ ] Self-review: every "Action Required" item has a regulatory cite and an actionable checklist

### 1 week before publish
- [ ] Final edit pass

- [ ] Convert to PDF
- [ ] Upload to subscriber portal

### Publish day
- [ ] Confirm delivery to all tiers (Starter FAA-only, Professional all sections)
- [ ] Check portal archive link works
- [ ] LinkedIn post (teaser: top 3 findings this quarter)
