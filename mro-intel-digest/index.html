The Problem

Regulatory changes don't announce themselves to your inbox.

A MAG Change 10 lands. A CCAR-145 training requirement updates. UK CAA enforcement priorities shift. Your EASA certificate lapses because nobody told you the renewal cycle changed. Your quality manager is too busy auditing to monitor five regulatory authorities at once.

🔁 Rapid Change Velocity

Between MAG changes, EASA rulemaking, FAA AC revisions, CAAC AMC updates, and CASA EOs, the regulatory landscape shifts constantly. Missing one update can mean a citation at your next surveillance audit.

🌐 Multi-Jurisdiction Complexity

Dual-approved and multi-authority repair stations can't afford to track each authority separately. Most MRO quality managers have deep expertise in 1–2 authorities, not 5.

⏱️ No Time for Primary Research

Reading raw regulatory text is a full-time job. Quality managers need an expert to pre-read, filter, and translate — so they can spend time on implementation, not surveillance of the rulemaking.

💸 Compliance Surprises Are Expensive

An EASA Form 8 finding costs real money in corrective action, re-audit fees, and — worst case — temporary suspension of bilateral approval. Early warning is far cheaper.

Why this digest exists

The insight you'd get if you could call a lead auditor every quarter.

Most regulatory intelligence products cover one jurisdiction. Most QMS software covers one standard. But aviation MRO quality managers — especially those with bilateral approvals — have to track five authorities with completely different update cadences, different compliance cultures, and increasingly divergent requirements since Brexit.

I built this digest because I see the same pattern every time I audit: the finding that stings most isn't the one you knew about and deferred — it's the one that landed quietly in a regulatory update six months ago and nobody on the team caught it. This digest closes that gap.

Each issue is my professional read on what changed, what it means for your operation, and what you should do about it. No marketing fluff. No keyword-stuffed summaries. Just what a lead auditor would tell you if you called.

FAQ

Common questions

How often is the digest published?

Quarterly — January, April, July, October — covering the prior quarter's regulatory activity. Significant mid-quarter developments are delivered as brief flash alerts between issues.

What format is delivery?

Professional and Team subscribers receive PDF and Markdown versions. Starter receives PDF only. All issues are archived in the subscriber portal.

I only care about FAA and EASA — is Professional worth it?

For most dual-approved repair stations (FAA Part 145 + EASA Part-145 bilateral), Professional is the right tier. The cross-authority radar and MAG change tracking alone typically justify the upgrade from Starter.

Is this legal or regulatory advice?

No. This digest is professional analysis from a lead auditor, not legal advice. Always verify regulatory citations against current authority publications and consult your legal and compliance team before acting on any finding.

Can I share this with my team?

Individual subscriptions (Starter, Professional) are for one named subscriber. For team sharing, use the Team plan (up to 10 seats with shared portal access).

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel before your next quarterly billing cycle and you won't be charged again. You retain archive access through the end of your paid period.
