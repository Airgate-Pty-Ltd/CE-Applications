About This Tool

An EASA Production Organisation Approval (POA) under Part 21 Subpart G is required for manufacturers who wish to produce and release aircraft, engines, propellers, or parts to EASA airworthiness standards. The POA requires approval of the Production Organisation Exposition (POE), a Production Assurance System (PAS), and documented conformity inspection arrangements. This self-assessment covers the core POA requirements an EASA Production Organisation Inspector (POI) evaluates during initial approval, continued surveillance, and POA renewal visits.

Critical items (CRITICAL) represent Level 1 findings under EASA Part 21 — findings that constitute a significant non-compliance with requirements that jeopardise safety or invalidates the POA. A single CRITICAL NO forces a NOT READY verdict regardless of overall score.

Note: This tool covers the EASA-side requirements under Part 21 Subpart G. It is complementary to, but distinct from, FAA Part 21 PAH requirements. Organisations holding both EASA POA and FAA PC/PMA should use both tools.

Section 1 — Production Organisation Exposition (POE)

The POE (Production Organisation Exposition) is the cornerstone document of the EASA POA. It must describe the organisation's structure, management system, quality assurance, and procedures. The POE must be approved by the Competent Authority (EASA or NAA) and kept current. Reference: Part 21 21.A.143, AMC 21.A.143.
Critical — single NO = NOT READY (Level 1 finding basis)
Standard (Level 2 / OFI finding basis)
POE-1Does the organisation maintain a current, approved Production Organisation Exposition (POE) that has been accepted by the Competent Authority, and is the POE revision status controlled and accessible to relevant personnel?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.143 / AMC 21.A.143 POE content requirements / Level 1 finding basis
POE-2Does the POE describe: scope of work and products/parts covered; the Accountable Manager and their statement of commitment; the organisation chart; the production assurance system; quality procedures; and conformity inspection arrangements?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.143(a) / AMC 21.A.143 required elements / Level 1 if POE is absent or does not cover all required areas
POE-3Is there a documented process for amending the POE, including Competent Authority notification/approval requirements for significant changes, with amendment records showing CA acceptance?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.143(b) POE amendment / AMC 21.A.143 change management
POE-4Are there up-to-date procedures in the POE for each significant production activity, and are these procedures used by personnel in daily operations (not shelfware)?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.143(a)(6) / AMC 21.A.143 procedures list

Section 2 — Accountable Manager & Personnel

The Accountable Manager (AM) is personally accountable to the Competent Authority for the organisation's compliance with Part 21 Subpart G. The AM must be empowered to commit resources and has specific Part 21 responsibilities that cannot be fully delegated. Key personnel must be identified and meet qualification requirements.
AM-1Is an Accountable Manager formally appointed (named in the POE), directly accessible to the Competent Authority, and empowered to ensure the organisation can finance and carry out production to applicable standards?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.145(c) / AMC 21.A.145 Accountable Manager requirements / Level 1 if AM not identified or not empowered
AM-2Are nominated managers (Quality Manager, Production Manager, etc.) identified in the POE with their qualifications, responsibilities, and lines of authority documented?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.145(b) / AMC 21.A.145 management structure
AM-3Is the Quality function independent from production with authority to halt production and reject conformity releases when non-compliance is identified?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.145(d) QA independence / AMC 21.A.145
AM-4Are all production and inspection personnel trained and competent for their tasks, with training records on file and a competency assessment process in place?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.145(b)(4) / AMC 21.A.145 personnel qualification

Section 3 — Conformity Inspection & Design Data

The POA holder must have approved design data (from the TC/STC/ETSO holder or own approved data) and must inspect all production to confirm conformity. Conformity inspection arrangements are a primary EASA POI audit focus.
CI-1Is current approved design data (TC, STC, ETSO, or design organisation approved data) identified, controlled, and accessible at points of production and inspection? Is the current revision status confirmed before use?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.133(b) / 21.A.143(a)(2) design data access / Level 1 if approved data not controlled or not available
CI-2Is a conformity inspection performed for each product/part produced, confirming conformance to the approved design data, with a conformity record (manufacturing order, traveler, or equivalent) generated and retained?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.163(b) / AMC 21.A.163 conformity inspection records / Level 1 if no conformity records
CI-3Are design changes (including production deviations and repairs) evaluated to determine whether they require design organisation or Competent Authority approval before implementation?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.133(c) / 21.A.163 configuration control / AMC to Part 21 Subpart G change management
CI-4Is there a documented process for managing concessions (production deviations/permits) requiring design organisation approval, with records of approved concessions retained?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.133(c) / POE deviation/concession management requirement

Section 4 — Airworthiness Release (EASA Form 1)

POA holders issue EASA Form 1 (Authorised Release Certificate) for parts, appliances, and components produced under the POA. Incorrect, incomplete, or unauthorised EASA Form 1 issuance is a Level 1 finding that can trigger POA suspension.
EF1-1Is there a documented, controlled process for issuing EASA Form 1 (Authorised Release Certificate), with authorised signatories identified and their authorisation documented, and signatories trained on EASA Form 1 completion requirements?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.163(b) / Appendix I EASA Form 1 completion instructions / Level 1 if unauthorised signatories or Form 1 issued incorrectly
EF1-2Does each EASA Form 1 issued correctly complete: blocks 12 (status/work performed), 13a (remarks including conformity statement), 14a (EASA Form 1 statement), 14b (regulation), 14c (signature/stamp), 14d (certificate number), 14e (POA number), 14f (date)?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 Appendix I EASA Form 1 / AMC to Part 21 Appendix I Form 1 completion / Level 1 if blocks 14a-f incorrect
EF1-3Are copies of issued EASA Form 1 certificates retained and linked to the conformity records for the corresponding product/part lot, and are records accessible for Competent Authority review?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.163(a) record retention / AMC 21.A.163

Section 5 — Production Assurance System (PAS) & Internal Audit

The Production Assurance System (PAS) is the POA's equivalent of a QMS — it must demonstrate systematic control over all production processes. Internal independent monitoring (audit) is a mandatory POA element. Reference: Part 21 21.A.139.
PAS-1Does the organisation maintain a Production Assurance System (PAS) that monitors conformity with applicable requirements, identifies non-compliance findings, and ensures corrective action is taken and verified effective?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.139 / AMC 21.A.139 PAS requirements / Level 1 if PAS absent or not functional
PAS-2Does the PAS include an independent monitoring (audit) function that audits all areas of the POA's production activities, with findings reported to the Accountable Manager and records of audits and corrective actions maintained?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.139(e) independent monitoring / AMC 21.A.139 monitoring function / Level 1 if monitoring function absent
PAS-3Is there a procedure for reporting safety-significant findings (airworthiness-relevant non-conformances) to the Competent Authority, the design organisation, and/or EASA, with records of notifications made?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.3A occurrence reporting / AMC 21.A.3A / AMC 21.A.139
PAS-4Are non-conformities identified in the PAS tracked through to root cause and corrective action closure, with the Accountable Manager briefed on systemic issues?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.139(d) corrective action / AMC 21.A.139

Section 6 — Suppliers & Subcontractors

The POA holder is responsible for the airworthiness of all parts produced under the POA, including those manufactured by subcontractors. Subcontractor oversight is a common EASA POI finding area. Reference: Part 21 21.A.133(a), 21.A.143(a)(7).
SUB-1Are subcontractors/suppliers who manufacture parts or perform processes for the POA holder listed in the POE (or an approved procedure/list), with their scope of work and control mechanism documented?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.133(a) / 21.A.143(a)(7) supplier listing / AMC 21.A.143 subcontractor approval process
SUB-2Are flow-down requirements (applicable design data, inspection requirements, airworthiness release/C of C requirements) included in purchase orders or contractual agreements with subcontractors?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.133(a) / AMC 21.A.139 supplier oversight flow-down
SUB-3Is receiving inspection performed on subcontractor-supplied items before release to production, with inspection records demonstrating the check was performed?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.163(b) / AMC 21.A.163 receiving inspection as part of conformity inspection

Section 7 — Facilities, Equipment & Materials

The POA holder must have adequate facilities, equipment, tools, and materials to ensure conformity with applicable requirements. Reference: Part 21 21.A.145(a).
FAC-1Are production facilities adequate for the scope of work (covered by the POA), and is the facility description in the POE current and accurate including any additional/satellite locations?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.145(a)(1) / AMC 21.A.145 facility requirements
FAC-2Are measuring and test equipment used in conformity inspection calibrated, with calibration records traceable to national or international measurement standards, and are out-of-calibration instruments removed from service?CRITICAL
Ref: Part 21 21.A.145(a)(3) / AMC 21.A.145 calibration requirements / Level 1 basis if uncalibrated equipment used in release
FAC-3Are raw materials and bought-out parts received and stored to prevent deterioration, with shelf-life and storage condition requirements controlled?
Ref: Part 21 21.A.145(a)(2) / AMC 21.A.145 material control
