About This Tool

Production Approval Holders (PAHs) — including Parts Manufacturer Approval (PMA), Technical Standard Order Authorization (TSOA), and Production Certificate (PC) holders — are subject to FAA ASI (Aviation Safety Inspector) surveillance under 14 CFR Part 21. This self-assessment covers the core compliance requirements across all three PAH types, including design data control, conformity inspection, quality system, supplier oversight, record retention, and airworthiness release.

Each question maps to a specific 14 CFR Part 21 requirement. Critical items (CRITICAL) represent findings FAA ASIs most commonly cite as unsafe conditions or enforcement actions. A single CRITICAL NO forces a NOT COMPLIANT verdict regardless of overall score.

Section 1 — Quality System Requirements

Applies to all PAH types. The PAH must maintain a quality system that ensures each article conforms to its type design (or approved data for TSOs). The quality system must be documented and demonstrate effective control. Reference: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1), 21.143, 21.607; AC 21-43.

QS-1Is the PAH's quality system documented in a Quality Manual or equivalent document that covers: organization, responsibilities, conformity inspection process, supplier control, record retention, and corrective action?CRITICAL

Section 2 — Design Data & Conformity

A PAH must manufacture to approved design data. For PMA: identicality, test and computation, or STC-basis approved data (21.303(a)). For TSO: TSOA minimum performance standard (MPS) data. For PC: approved type design data. Design data control is the most common ASI finding category.
DD-1Is the current approved design data (PMA supplement, TSOA letter/MPS, or TC/STC data package) identified, controlled, and accessible to production and inspection? Is the current revision status known and retrievable?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(b)(1) (PMA) / 21.607(a) (TSO) / 21.143(c)(1) (PC) / AC 21.303-4 §7 design data requirements
DD-2Is there a documented process to ensure that only current, approved design data (drawings, specifications, engineering orders) is used in production — with superseded data removed or clearly identified as obsolete?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(i) / AC 21-43 §5.2 document control / AS9100D Cl. 7.5 (if aligned)
DD-3Does each article produced have a completed conformity record (traveler, inspection record, or equivalent) showing it was inspected to, and conforms with, the approved design data?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(iii) / 21.147(a) (PC) / AC 21-43 §5.4 conformity inspection records
DD-4Is there a process for managing engineering changes — including determining whether a change requires revised approved data (FAA approval) vs. can be made under the PAH's own authority — with change records maintained?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(f) design change PMA / 21.113 major vs. minor change / AC 21.303-4 §9 change management
DD-5Are drawings and specifications used in production marked with current revision level, and is the revision level traceable to the approved design data basis?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(i) / AC 21-43 §5.2

Section 3 — Supplier & Subcontractor Control

The PAH is responsible for the airworthiness of all articles it produces, including those manufactured by suppliers. 14 CFR Part 21 flow-down to suppliers is a high-priority ASI finding area, particularly for PMA holders with offshore or low-tier suppliers.
SC-1Is there a documented supplier control program that identifies approved suppliers, defines flow-down requirements (design data, inspection requirements, airworthiness certifications), and includes a mechanism for supplier approval and re-evaluation?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(iv) / AC 21-43 §5.6 supplier control / FAA enforcement: inadequate supplier oversight
SC-2Are purchase orders or supplier contracts documented with flow-down of: applicable design data revision, inspection requirements, Certificate of Conformance (C of C) requirements, FAA approval basis, and right of access for PAH and FAA inspection?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(iv) / AC 21.303-4 §8 supplier flow-down / FAA Order 8120.22 supplier oversight expectations
SC-3Is receiving inspection performed on supplier-provided articles, with inspection criteria defined and records showing the inspection was completed before articles entered production?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(iii) / AC 21-43 §5.4 receiving inspection
SC-4Are supplier C of Cs (Certificates of Conformance) retained for each incoming lot, confirming conformance to specification and approved design data?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(iii)(iv) / AC 21-43 §5.4
SC-5Is there a process for qualifying, monitoring, and removing suppliers from the approved supplier list based on quality performance (incoming rejection rate, corrective action compliance)?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(iv) / AC 21-43 §5.6 supplier evaluation

Section 4 — Airworthiness Release & Certificates

PAHs must issue appropriate airworthiness approval tags or C of Cs for articles they produce. For PMA and TSO articles: FAA Form 8130-3 or C of C per 21.303(a)(2). For PC articles: aircraft/engine log book entries or 8130-3. Improper or missing airworthiness release is a Category 1 FAA finding.
AR-1Is there a documented, controlled process for issuing airworthiness releases (FAA Form 8130-3 or authorized C of C) for PAH-produced articles, with authorized signatories identified and their authorization documented?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(a)(2) (PMA) / 21.611 (TSO) / 21.147(b) (PC) / AC 21-43 §5.7 / FAA Form 8130-3 completion requirements
AR-2Does the airworthiness release / C of C correctly identify: article part number, revision level, serial number (if applicable), production approval number (PMA letter / TSOA number / PC number), conformance to approved data, and release date?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(a)(2) / AC 21.303-4 Appendix 4 (C of C content) / FAA Form 8130-3 instructions
AR-3Are copies of issued 8130-3 forms or C of Cs retained in the PAH's records and linked to the conformity records for each shipped lot?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(v) / AC 21-43 §5.8 record retention
AR-4Is there a process to prevent unauthorized personnel from issuing airworthiness approvals, with a current list of authorized signatories reviewed at least annually?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(ii) / AC 21-43 §5.7 signatory control

Section 5 — Record Retention & Traceability

14 CFR Part 21 specifies minimum record retention periods. The FAA may request records during an ASI surveillance visit at any time. Missing or incomplete records result in an inability to demonstrate compliance and typically result in an enforcement finding.
RR-1Are production conformity records retained for a minimum of 2 years (PMA) or per the applicable requirement, with records showing: date of production, article identification, conformity check results, inspector identity, and disposition?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(v) (PMA) / 21.147(c)(2) (PC) / AC 21-43 §5.8 minimum retention periods
RR-2Is traceability maintained from finished article to raw material and inspection records, so that a suspect lot can be identified and isolated if a production problem is discovered after shipment?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(c)(1)(iii) / AC 21-43 §5.4 traceability / FAA Corrective Action applicability
RR-3Are customer-complaint and airworthiness-concern records retained and do they include follow-up action taken, including notification to FAA when a safety-of-flight issue is identified?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.3 reporting requirements / AC 21-43 §5.5 / FAA voluntary disclosure programs

Section 6 — PMA-Specific Requirements (Subpart K / 21.303)

Additional requirements specific to Parts Manufacturer Approval holders. Covers PMA supplement content, design approval basis, and continued airworthiness responsibilities unique to 14 CFR 21.303.
PMA-1Is the PAH's PMA supplement (the formal FAA-issued approval document) current — covering all part numbers and configurations being produced — and is the supplement accessible to inspectors at the point of production?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(a) / AC 21.303-4 §6 PMA supplement format and content / FAA FSIMS 8120.22
PMA-2For each PMA part number, is the design approval basis documented (identicality / test and computation / STC-based) and is the supporting data (identicality findings, test reports, or STC reference) on file and readily retrievable?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(b) design approval basis / AC 21.303-4 §7 identicality / §8 test and computation data requirements
PMA-3If the PMA basis includes identicality, is there documented evidence (dimensional reports, material certifications, test reports) that the PMA part is identical in design to the TC holder part for all safety-of-flight characteristics?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.303(b)(3) identicality / AC 21.303-4 Appendix 2 identicality data package
PMA-4Is there a process for identifying and notifying the FAA if an unsafe condition is discovered in a PMA article (reporting under 14 CFR 21.3), with records of notifications made?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.3 reporting of failures / AC 21.303-4 §11 continued airworthiness responsibilities

Section 7 — TSOA-Specific Requirements (Subpart O / 21.607+)

Additional requirements specific to Technical Standard Order Authorization holders. Each article must meet the Minimum Performance Standards (MPS) defined in the applicable TSO (TSO-C105, TSO-C119, etc.) and pass the qualification testing documented in the TSO design approval.
TSO-1Is the current TSOA letter and all associated data (qualification test reports, approved design data, TSOA deviation requests) on file and controlled, with copies accessible to production and QA?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.607 / 21.611(a) TSOA content / FAA TSOA issuance letter content requirements
TSO-2Does each TSO article produced pass all production acceptance tests required by the TSO and applicable MPS, with test records generated and retained per article/lot?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.611(c) / TSO MPS acceptance test requirements / AC 21-43 §5.4
TSO-3Is there a process to ensure that any design change to a TSOA article is evaluated for impact on the TSOA — distinguishing between changes requiring FAA approval vs. changes that can be made under the TSOA holder's authority?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.619 TSOA design changes / AC 21-43 change control guidance

Section 8 — PC Holder Requirements (Subpart G / 21.143+)

Production Certificate holders produce aircraft, engines, or propellers under an approved quality system inspected by the FAA. PC holders must demonstrate their quality system covers all production activities and that the Compliance Program is effective.
PC-1Is the PC holder's quality system description (QSD) or equivalent document current, FAA-accepted, and covering all production activities and manufacturing locations authorized under the PC?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.143 / AC 21-43 §5 PC quality system / FAA Order 8120.22 PC holder requirements
PC-2For each aircraft/engine/propeller produced, is there a completed conformity file showing compliance with the approved type design for all critical inspection stages, signed off by authorized QA inspectors?CRITICAL
Ref: 14 CFR 21.147 / AC 21-43 §5.4 PC conformity file requirements
PC-3Is the FAA notified of production rate changes, new manufacturing locations, or significant quality system changes as required by the PC?
Ref: 14 CFR 21.143(d) notification requirements / AC 21-43 §6 FAA notification
